There is one enrichment stream in the Risk Management Science Programme: Risk Analytics Stream.  Students who got a total score of 30 or above in the best five HKDSE subjects or have obtained a cumulative GPA of 3.7 or above in the first or second year of attendance may apply for specializing in the Risk Analytics Stream.
