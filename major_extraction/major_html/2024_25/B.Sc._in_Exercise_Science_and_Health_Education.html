Explanatory Notes:

 

1.

SPED courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major Programme Requirement as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students who have completed 3 units of Core Skills Courses will be eligible to apply for exemption of 1 unit of University Core Physical Education Requirement.

Students are required to apply for the exemption. When exemption from a particular course is recognized, students can only be exempted from the course but not the units.
