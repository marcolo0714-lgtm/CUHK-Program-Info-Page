Major Programme Requirement

 

Students are required to complete a minimum of 72 units of courses as follows:

 

 

Units

1.

Faculty Package:

9

 

CURE1400 [#CUMT1000] and any two courses from:

 

 

ANTH1020, ARTS1005, 1009, 1010, BMBL1001, CHES1101 or 1102, CHLL1900 or 1902 or 1903, CURE1000, 1110, ENGE1000, FAAS1900, HIST1000 or 1700 or 1701, JASP1090, LING1000, MUSC1000, PHIL1110, THEO1000, TRAN1000

 

 

 

 

2.

Required Courses:

 18

 

CUMT1002, CURE1401 [#CUMT1001], CURE1403 [#CUMT1003], CURE4401[#CUMT4001], MGNT1020, MKTG2010

 

 

 

 

3.

Elective Courses[a]:

 45

(a)

Any 9 units from Area 1

 

(b)

Any 9 units from Area 3

 

(c)

Any 12 units from Area 4[b]

 

(d)

Any 12 units from CUMT2001, 2002, 2013, 4005, 4102, 4103, CURE2403 [#CUMT2003], CURE2404 [#CUMT2004], CURE2405 [#CUMT2005], CURE2406 [#CUMT2006], CURE2407 [#CUMT2007], CURE2408, CURE2409 [#CUMT2009], CURE2410 [#CUMT2010], CURE2411 [#CUMT3001], CURE2412 [#CUMT3002], CURE3401, CURE3402, CURE3403 [#CUMT3003], CURE3404 [#CUMT3004], CURE3405 [#CUMT3005], CURE3406, CURE3407 [#CUMT3007], CURE3408 [#CUMT3008], CURE3409 [#CUMT3009], CURE3411, CURE3508 [#CUMT3108], CURE4403 [#CUMT4003], CURE4404 [#CUMT4004], CURE4405, CURE4408 [#CUMT4008], CURE4411 [#CUMT4101], CURE4421, CURE4431 [#CUMT4201], CURE4441 [#CUMT4301], with at least 9 units at 2000 or above level[c]

 

(e)

Capstone Courses: CUMT4102 or 4103

 

 

Total:

72

Explanatory Notes:

1.

ANTH, CUMT, CURE, ENGE, FAAS, JASP, MGNT, MKTG courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

Courses which count towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 1. Faculty Package and 2. Required Courses above cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 3. Elective Courses.

[b]

 

Students can declare a concentration in one of the following concentrations/special studies if they successfully complete 12 units from the same concentration:

i.            Antiquities and Heritage Management;

ii.          Cultural Institutions and Curation; and

iii.        Cultural and Creative Industries

[c]

 

Courses which count towards the fulfillment of the elective course requirements specified in 3(a), 3(b), 3(c) and 3 (e) cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the elective course requirements specified in 3(d).

[#]

Old course code used in 2024-25 and before.
