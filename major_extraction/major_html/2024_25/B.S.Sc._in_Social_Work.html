Explanatory Notes:

1.

SOWK courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

For students transferred to the Social Work Programme and who had taken SOWK1113 or 1114 as part of the Faculty Package, the courses should be counted as part of their elective courses. They should take an additional Faculty Package course to fulfill the Faculty Package requirement.

3.

Only a number of seminar courses will be offered in each academic year in accordance with resources available.

4.

All social work students are required to take at least one PSYC course and one SOCI course, including the ones listed under the Faculty Package.

[a]

Students are encouraged to take SOWK4030 preferably in their final year of study to enhance their research ability by conducting a guided research on selected topics.
