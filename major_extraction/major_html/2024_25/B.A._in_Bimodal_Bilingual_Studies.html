Explanatory Notes:

1.

BMBL, CHED, EDUC, HKSL, LING, SLPA, SOWK and TRAN courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major Programme Requirement will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

For students whose native language is Hong Kong Sign Language (HKSL) or who possess equivalent competence in HKSL, subject to the approval of the Major Department, they should take course(s) of a language other than English, Chinese and HKSL, and LING courses in place of the HKSL courses, as specified below:
