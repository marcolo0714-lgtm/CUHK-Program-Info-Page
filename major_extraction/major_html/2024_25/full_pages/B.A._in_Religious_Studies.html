Explanatory Notes:

1.

ANTH, ARAB, CURE, EDUC, PSYC and THEO courses at 2000 and above level (except those marked with *) as included in the respective concentrations will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification for students specialize in the relevant concentrations.

2.

Courses counted towards the fulfillment of one of the above listed requirements cannot be used to fulfill concentration requirements.

[a]

Excluding the courses taken to fulfil requirement 1 and 2 above.

[b]

For students not specialising in any concentration, apart from the THEO courses listed in the course list, students may take other THEO courses, subject to a maximum of five THEO courses, as their elective courses to fulfill requirement 3(a).

[c]

For Cultural Studies Areas courses, please refer to the course list of Cultural Studies Programme.

[ ]

Old course code used in 2024-25 and before.
