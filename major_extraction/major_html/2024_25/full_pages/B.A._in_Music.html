Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

 

1.

Major Programme:

Knowledge Outcomes (Content):

Graduates will have possessed fundamental/core/inter-disciplinary knowledge of music, including the following:

(1) music theory, (2) contextual study of music (ethnomusicological, historical, etc.), (3) performance on a major instrument, (4) multiple musical traditions.

Skills Outcomes:

Generic Competencies/Transferable Skills:

Graduates will have acquired transferable generic skills, including: (1) ability to work independently and in groups, (2) ability to express themselves clearly in written and spoken English and Chinese, (3) ability to think creatively and critically.

Professional Skills:

Graduates will have gained a good mastery of the following professional skills: (1) ability to express themselves about music in written and spoken Chinese and English, (2) ability to make evidence-based critical judgments about musical performance, composition, and musical culture, (3) ability to perform at an appropriate professional level on their major instrument.

Attitude/Value Outcomes

Graduates will have developed a positive attitude towards issues concerning music, including the following: (1) awareness of and open-minded attitude toward different modes of musical expression, (2) passion for communicating through music, and (3) awareness of and appreciation for the significance of music within all societies.

2.

Minor Programme:

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
