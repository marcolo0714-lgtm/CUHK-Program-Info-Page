GLEF courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled with # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

 

2.

GLEF majors are not allowed to declare minor in Economics.

 

3.

Double majors in GLEF and ECON, GLEF and IFAA, and GLEF and QFIN are not allowed.

 

4.

Students of GLEF Programme who wish to claim IBBA as second major must choose a concentration area other than the Finance concentration area.

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
GLEFN_Learning Outcomes_ENG

 

1.    Major Programme:

 

Upon completion of the Programme, students will have:

Knowledge outcomes (K) 

  • acquired an understanding of the complex interrelationships among the world economies in terms of the flow of goods, services, and funds. (K1)
  • studied the functions and operations of financial markets and institutions, and the interaction among them in a globalised context. (K2) 
  • analysed the wide range of financial instruments in the globalised economies with regard to their design, properties, and pricing. (K3) 

 Skills outcomes (S)

  • equipped students with the ability to use econometric and mathematical modeling tools in order to: 

(a) understand and analyse emerging trends in global financial markets and institutions, 
(b) support financial decision making at a corporate or individual level, and
(c) formulate global strategies for business and public organisations in an increasingly complex financial landscape. (S1) 

  • developed generic skills of communication in verbal and written form and of collaboration with peers in a team work environment. (S2)

Values outcomes (V) 

Nurtured virtues that are conducive to good personal ethics, such as: 

  • Honesty and integrity (V1)
  • Cross-cultural sensitivity and tolerance (V2)
  • Capacity of life-long learning. (V3)
