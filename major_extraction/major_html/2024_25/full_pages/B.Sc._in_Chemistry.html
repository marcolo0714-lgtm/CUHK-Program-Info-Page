Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

1.

Major Programme:

 

I.

Chemistry

 

 

A.

Knowledge Outcomes

Graduates should possess fundamental knowledge of the following:

(1)

chemistry principles, concepts, and applications of inorganic chemistry, organic chemistry, physical chemistry, and analytical chemistry; and

(2)

mathematical techniques required in chemical science.

 

 

Graduates should have the ability of applying the acquired knowledge in exploring chemistry-related problems.

 

 

B.

Skills Outcomes

Professional Skills

Graduates should have a good mastery of professional skills, including the following:

(1)

ability of recognizing key factors and applying appropriate assumption to formulate solutions for chemistry problems;

(2)

basic mathematical skills for handling experimental data and quantitative arguments in physical chemistry;

(3)

ability of determining molecular structure based on spectroscopic measurements;

(4)

ability to implement safety measures in conducting chemical laboratory works;

(5)

ability to operate instruments in chemistry laboratories;

(6)

ability in carrying out prescribed procedures using suited experimental skills and instrumentation;

(7)

ability to conduct literature reviews/experimental works and to report scientific findings in both oral and written formats; and

(8)

competence in using commercial computer software for graphical depiction of molecular systems.

 

 

Graduates should be able to investigate chemistry disciplines using combinations of the above skills.

 

Generic Skills

Graduates should have acquired the following generic skills:

(1)

problem solving skills including ability to identify key issues and formulate feasible solutions of problems based on logical analysis and critical thinking;

(2)

ability to sort out complex observations to construct logical proposition;

(3)

ability of formulating logical sequence in arguments;

(4)

critical evaluation of issues based on evidence and scientific logic;

(5)

ability of analyzing issues quantitatively with the sense of level of uncertainty and reliability;

(6)

ability to acquire new knowledge by self-study and to conduct independent work with logical planning;

(7)

interpersonal skills including the ability to work effectively in a group;

(8)

information retrieval skills through library and internet; and

(9)

time management skills.

 

 

C.

Attitude/Value Outcomes

Graduates should have developed the following attitudes and values:

(1)

appreciation of the beauty of chemistry and its importance as a central science;

(2)

curiosity and interest in scientific exploration;

(3)

awareness of impacts of chemistry in environmental, social, economical, industrial, and technological aspects;

(4)

awareness of chemical safety;

(5)

confidence in ability of solving problems;

(6)

motivation and ability for life-long learning; and

(7)

sense of taking responsibility in study and work environments.

Minor Programme:

 

Upon the completion of the Chemistry Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to Chemistry such as Analytical Chemistry, Inorganic Chemistry, Organic Chemistry and Physical Chemistry. They will have acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subjects with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in other areas in Science.

 

While most skills and attitudes expected of a graduate would have been developed through the Major Programme, graduates who have been awarded a Minor will have developed the basic technical skills and attitudes towards issues in Chemistry. They will also be aware of the latest developments in Chemistry, and the impact of Chemistry in social, industrial, environmental and technological contexts.
