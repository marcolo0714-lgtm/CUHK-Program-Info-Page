Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
RELS

1.

Major programme:

Knowledge:

Graduates will have acquired the following:

(1)

The understanding of religion in its historical settings and contemporary contexts of modern living, globally and locally.

(2)

The understanding of religion in its different dimensions.

(3)

The understanding of religion through a crtical analysis.

(4)

A basic knowledge of major religious traditions in the world.

Skills:

Graduates will have acquired the following:

(1)

Communication and presentational (oral and written) skills.

(2)

Analytical and problem solving skills.

(3)

Social skills and team-work skills.

(4)

Skills of critical intelligence and appreciative thinking.

(5)

Skills in cross-cultural understanding and intercultural literacy.

(6)

Research skills.

Attitude:

Graduates will have acquired the following:

(1)

Awareness of the plurality and multi-faceted complexity within religious traditions of the issues of multiple and conflicting interpretations of religious traditions.

(2)

Self-critical awareness of pre-suppositions and recognition.

(3)

A critical and yet sympathetic understanding and appreciation of religious traditions especially those to which one does not belong.

(4)

Sensitivity to the ultimate concern and the religious dimension of human life.

 

 

2.

Minor programme:

1.       Cultural Studies

Upon the completion of the Minor programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career

 

2.       Film and Screen Studies

Upon completion of the programme, students will have acquired the following learning outcomes in three aspects:

 

I.               Knowledge outcomes:

a.              Demonstrate a critical appreciation of some of the dominant concepts, methods, and debates informing the study of film and screen.

b.              Demonstrate a capacity to analyze the form and content of particular audio-visual products.

c.              Demonstrate an awareness of the variety of ways in which audio-visual products and aspects of individual and collective social lives are mutually constituted.

d.              Demonstrate an understanding of different traditions of film and screen practices in different national and international contexts.

 

II.            Skills outcomes:

a.              Demonstrate skills in the close formal and structural analysis of different kinds of audio- visual products.

b.              Demonstrate skills in the research and evaluation of relevant critical and historical materials for the study of film and screen.

c.              Demonstrate an ability to interrelate texts and discourses specific to their own discipline with issues in the wider context of cultural and intellectual history.

 

d.              Demonstrate an ability to understand and analyze relevant theoretical ideas and to apply these ideas to audio-visual forms.

 

III.          Attitude outcomes:

 

a.              Through seminar work and group presentations, demonstrate communication skills and an ability to work both individually and in groups.

b.              Acknowledge the contested and provisional nature of knowledge and understanding and show openness towards diverse positions and respect for differing views.
