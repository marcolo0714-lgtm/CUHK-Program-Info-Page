Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
CLEDN_Learning Outcomes_ENG

 

1.

Major Programme:

Graduates from the programme will:

(1)

Demonstrate a mastery of the Chinese language and literature to a level that meets the expectations for quality teaching in schools;

(2)

Attain an advanced level of proficiency in both Cantonese and Putonghua expression for the purpose of effective teaching of the Chinese language;

(3)

Develop knowledge and skills relating to Chinese language teaching;

(4)

Develop practical skills in material design and adaptation, in the use of textbooks and other resources, in the assessment of language ability, and in curriculum design and evaluation.
