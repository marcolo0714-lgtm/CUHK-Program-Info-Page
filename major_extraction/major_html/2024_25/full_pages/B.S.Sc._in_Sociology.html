SOCI courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students who pursue a second Major in Journalism and Communication can complete COMM1120, SOCI1001 and one Faculty Package course offered by a Programme/Department/School other than SOCI and COMM to satisfy the Faculty Package course requirements of both Major Programmes.

Students who pursue a second Major in Journalism and Communication can be exempted from taking COMM2160 by taking SOCI2003 as the units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

4.

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
Learning outcome

1.         

Major Programme:

 

By successfully completing our major programme, the sociology student should be able to:

 

Knowledge Domain

(1)

understand the role of Sociology in contributing to our knowledge of social order and its changes;

(2)

apply basic concepts derived from sociological theories in the understanding of social phenomenon;

(3)

pursue an area of concentration (China studies, social research, and sociology and liberal studies) whenever appropriate and prepare for his/her career development in Chinese management, applied social research and teaching;

(4)

interpret the developments of Chinese communities and their place in the global and regional context;

 

 

Skills Domain

(5)

master basic qualitative and quantitative methods in Sociology;

(6)

think critically;

(7)

develop transferable skills beyond disciplinary requirements;

 

 

Values Domain

(8)

reflect on his or her own value position and appreciate:

·             

sociology as a useful and meaningful perspective on social reality and individual biography;

·             

the importance of ethics, honesty, and transparency in research and academic settings; and

·             

the importance of sociological analyses for reflection of value positions and life choices.

 

 

2.

Sociology & Journalism and Communication Double Major Programme:

 

Upon completion of the Double Major Programme, students will have:

 

K = Knowledge outcomes

(1)

gained a breath of knowledge in Sociology, and Journalism and Communication

(2)

developed a high level of critical thinking and analytical reasoning skills through cross-disciplinary training

(3)

expanded and extended intellectual flexibility and diversity

(4)

developed knowledge essential to fostering global and regional sustainable development

(5)

harnessed the synergies between two influential social science disciplines

 

 

S = Skills outcomes

(1)

developed multiple skills in addressing complex world problems

(2)

acquired the ability to theorize, design and execute social research critically and independently

(3)

acquired the ability to formulate evidence-based responses for policy or professional communication

(4)

mastered informative and persuasive communication and writing skills

(5)

acquired analytical and innovative problem-solving abilities in preparation for Industry 4.0

 

 

V = Values and attitude outcomes

(1)

worked under the mentoring of senior academics and qualified practitioners

(2)

developed a strong sense of social consciousness, freedom of expression, and social responsibility towards achieving sustainable development

(3)

acquired the ability to instill a strong commitment to professional ethics in communication professions and in conducting social research

(4)

developed autonomy, responsibility and accountability in individual or collaborative work

 

 

3.

Minor Programme:

 

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
