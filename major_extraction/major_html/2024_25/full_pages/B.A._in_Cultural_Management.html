Explanatory Notes:

1.

ANTH, CUMT, CURE, ENGE, FAAS, JASP, MGNT, MKTG courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

Courses which count towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 1. Faculty Package and 2. Required Courses above cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 3. Elective Courses.

Students can declare a concentration in one of the following concentrations/special studies if they successfully complete 12 units from the same concentration:

i.            Antiquities and Heritage Management;

Courses which count towards the fulfillment of the elective course requirements specified in 3(a), 3(b), 3(c) and 3 (e) cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the elective course requirements specified in 3(d).

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

 

1.

Major Programme:

Upon completion of the Programme, students will have acquired the following:

Knowledge:

(1)

appreciation and interpretation of various arts forms

(2)

understanding of cultural history and heritages of HK, China and beyond

(3)

understanding of local and international issues in cultural management

(4)

understanding of the strengths and potentials of arts in a particular context

Skills-based:

(1)

organization and management skills

(2)

critical analytical skills and appreciation/ interpretation of various arts forms

(3)

creative skills to develop innovative approaches and shaping of cultural policy

(4)

multi-disciplinary study

Attitudes:

(1)

appreciation and respect for different cultures

(2)

commitment in developing local culture with a global vision
