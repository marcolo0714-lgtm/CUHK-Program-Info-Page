Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
Gerontology

1.          Major Programme:

Graduates from the Programme will have acquired the ability to:

 

Knowledge domain

  1. Describe the fundamental concepts in chronic disease management, aged care services design including psychological and societal perspectives of ageing
  2. Explain the theoretical models and frameworks related to care in gerontology and hospice care plans
  3. Describe the fundamental knowledge of epidemiology and biostatistics, infectious disease control in aged care services

 

Skill domain

  1. Optimise policies and financing strategies for aged care
  2. Coordinate aged services to improve efficiency
  3. Utilize evidence based approach to inquire, analyze and criticize contemporary public health issues
  4. Strengthen the infection control capacity of aged service facilities
  5. Steer quality aged service innovation
  6. Strengthen communications among practitioners, and provide high-quality and individualised services

 

Value domain

  1. Demonstrate respect for cultural diversity and sensitivity
  2. Demonstrate dedication to sustainable development
  3. Demonstrate care for social equity and justice
  4. Demonstrate understanding on ethical issues in ageing and end-of-life care
