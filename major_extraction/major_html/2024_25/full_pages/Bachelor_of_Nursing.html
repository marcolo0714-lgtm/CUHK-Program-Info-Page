Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completion of the programme, students are able to:

(1)   provide evidence-based care that supports physical and homeostatic functioning, promotes psychosocial functioning and life-style changes, protects against harm, recognizes the patients as a member of the family unit and community, and leverages on effective use of the health-care delivery system (K/S/V).

(2)   apply appropriate clinical and nursing therapeutic techniques unsupervised (K/S).

(3)   apply the principles of health promotion in meeting the health needs of individuals, groups, families and communities (K/S/V).

(4)   demonstrate effective skills in communicating with patients, carers, peers and the health care team (S).

(5)   coordinate the care of clients in hospital and community settings (K/S).

(6)   apply technology in the processing of information for nursing practice and research (K/S).

(7)   provide patient-centred care using the nursing process as a problem solving approach (K/S).

(8)   recognize, explain and manage health problems using basic, social, behavioural, clinical sciences and managerial principles (K/S).

(9)   display appropriate professional attitudes and apply relevant ethical and legal principles in the practice of nursing (K/V).

(10)   demonstrate competent decision-making, clinical reasoning and judgment skills to make decisions and take actions (S/V).

(11)   demonstrate understanding of self and engage in ongoing lifelong personal development (V).

(12)   recognize the roles of nurses within a health service institution and their influences on its development, and engage in the development of nursing as a health-care profession (K/V).

(13)   uphold the professional code of conduct (K/V).

(14)   incorporate cultural factors into the practice of nursing (V).

K = Knowledge outcomes      S = Skills outcomes      V = Values and attitudes outcomes
