Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
CHESB_Learning outcomes

1.          Major Programme:

Knowledge outcomes:

•          

A broad-based and integrated comprehension of the complexities of Chinese history, culture, languages and contemporary society, including the integrated realms of the economy, governance, and business.

•          

A high-level of knowledge acquired through the direct social and practical engagement with Chinese society through social-cultural “immersion” and “on-the-ground” experiential and social enterprise learning.

•          

A focused area of specific topical knowledge about China explored through intensive empirical research (mainly the capstone research sequence in the final year).

•          

An understanding and appreciation of the existing and developing multiple needs and

opportunities for the shared productive action of Chinese citizens and members of the international community together with respect to global challenges.

 

Skills outcomes:

•          

A high level of proficiency in Chinese language (Putonghua), including in reading and writing Chinese, at the level needed to be China-specialist analysts, effective cross-cultural communicators and social network builders linking China with the world.

•          

A high level of spoken and written English language ability to support effective communication in cross-cultural discussions about China in international arenas.

•          

A high level ability in critical thinking, rational analysis, logical persuasive argumentation, and original empirical research necessary for today’s China specialist analysts.

•          

A notable skill level at effective cross-cultural problem solving, negotiation, and joint enterprise development gained through the social-cultural “immersion” experience and “on-the-ground” in China experiential and social enterprise learning.

 

Values and attitude outcomes:

•          

A high level of cross-cultural respect, tolerance, appreciation and compassion, along with an attentiveness to reflection on essential social-ethical values, drawn from interactive student discussions, social-cultural immersion, experiential and social enterprise learning in China.
