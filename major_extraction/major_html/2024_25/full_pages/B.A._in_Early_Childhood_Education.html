Learning Outcomes
 
CHEDN_Learning Outcomes_ENG

 

1.

Major Programme:

Upon completion of the Programme, graduates of the B.A. Programme in Early Childhood Education will be able to:

1.

acquire knowledge on understanding the central concepts, tools of inquiry, and structures of early childhood education;

2.

design curriculum and instructional plans based on children's diverse abilities;

3.

apply a variety of instructional strategies to encourage the development of curiosity, creativity, problem-solving and performance skills in children;

4.

acquire knowledge and skills on a variety of formal and informal assessment strategies, and to use various assessment strategies to evaluate and ensure the continuous all-round development of young learners;

5.

demonstrate an understanding of the basic organizational and managerial theories, and ways to adopt appropriate strategies to contribute, independently and collegially, to the quality of their schools to the benefit of children, parents, the community;

6.

recognize the complex characteristics of young children, young children's families and communities, and to appreciate respectful and reciprocal relationships that support children's development and learning; and

7.

develop themselves to be innovative reflective practitioners who continually evaluate the effects of their choices and who actively research and implement new methods and seek out opportunities to grow professionally.
