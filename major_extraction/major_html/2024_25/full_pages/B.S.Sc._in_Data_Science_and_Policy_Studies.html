Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

 

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completion of their studies, students will have developed:

(1)

competency in knowledge of policy-making, data analytics, machine learning, Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence, information and communication technologies (ICT), statistics, engineering and relevant fields appropriate to the degree discipline;

(2)

intellectual and professional capacities to generate actionable social knowledge for deriving data-driven and evidence-based decisions with policy impacts;

(3)

the ability to use big data, computer, statistical and ICT technologies to conduct research and analysis, and to interpret results with understandings of their process, limitations and implications;

(4)

the ability to integrate and apply knowledge from an interdisciplinary and transdisciplinary approach to design and implement policy solutions;

(5)

the ability to combine analytical power with design thinking, creativity and innovation;

(6)

the ability to understand the technological, institutional and human dimensions of the impact of technologies and innovation on organizations and society;

(7)

the ability to understand professional and ethical responsibilities and social, economic, political, legal, and regulatory impacts of big data and policy change and innovation;

(8)

the ability to communicate data analysis, findings, and policy decisions to major stakeholders;

(9)

the ability to lead and work in teams in a global and collaborative context;

(10)

the understanding of entrepreneurship and citizenship;

(11)

an international perspective and a critical global mind-set; and

(12)

the ability of co-learning, co-creation of knowledge, and life-long learning.

 

 

2.          Minor Programme:

Upon completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated an understanding of fundamental issues related to policy studies and acquired basic skills in data science.  They will also have a sufficient understanding of how to make informed and good policy decisions based on data science and communicate such decisions to different stakeholders.  As data-driven and evidence-based policymaking can be broadly applied to different sectors to solve complex social problems, the Minor Programme can synergize with students’ major studies to enhance their competitiveness in career development.
