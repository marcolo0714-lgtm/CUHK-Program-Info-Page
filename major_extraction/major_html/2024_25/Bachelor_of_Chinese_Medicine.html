Major Programme Requirement

 

The Programme consists of two parts: the Pre-clinical Programme (4.5 years) and the Clinical Programme (1.5 years).

 

Pre-clinical Programme

In the Pre-clinical Programme (4.5 years), students have to complete both required and elective courses including subjects in basic medical and life sciences, Chinese medical concepts and principles of Chinese medicine application, Chinese medicine classics and history, specialties in Chinese medicine and clinical training.  Students who take “Clinical Training” courses (i.e. BCME 2401-2405) may need to undergo training in summer vacation.

 

Clinical Programme

The Clinical Programme extends over 1.5 years. The first year provides students with teaching in clinical settings of clinics and wards in a Chinese medicine hospital in the Mainland. Clinical training will cover various specialties in Chinese medicine including internal medicine, acupuncture and bone setting. Students will rotate among attachments to various clinical departments and write reports on case histories of selected topics. During the last six months, students will receive advanced clinical training in one or two selected specialties by attaching to a Chinese medicine hospital in the Mainland and/ or a specialty clinic in Hong Kong. They will also learn about the operation of Chinese medicine clinics in Hong Kong and the diagnosis and treatment of common medical problems seen in out-patient clinic settings. A professional examination will be arranged for students at the end of each term to assess their academic performance.  Students may need to undergo training during summer and winter vacations.
