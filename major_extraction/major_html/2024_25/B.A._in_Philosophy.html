Major Programme Requirement (for Associate Degree or Higher Diploma holders admitted to senior-year places)

 

Students are required to complete a minimum of 54 units of courses as follows:

 

 

Units

1.

Faculty Package:

3

 

PHIL1110*

 

 

 

 

2.

Required Courses:

24

 

PHIL1110*, 1310, 2020, 2030, 2040, 2050, 4800 and two courses from PHIL3000, 3800, 3820

 

 

 

 

3.

Elective Courses:

27

 

All Philosophy courses (including PHIL3000/3800/3820 not taken to fulfill requirement (2) above) on the course list other than the 27 units of required courses listed in (1) and (2) are elective courses. Students are required to take at least 3 units each from Areas 1, 2 and 3. Final year students are allowed to take a maximum of 6 units of courses from Area 5.

 

 

Total:

54

* Units of PHIL1110 counted as Faculty package units only.

 

Explanatory Notes:

1.

PHIL courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students are recommended to take PHIL1110, 1310, 2020, 2030, 2040 and 2050 in their first year of attendance.

3.

Students should obtain Grade “C-” or above in each of the courses of PHIL1110 and 1310. Otherwise, they are required to repeat the courses. The repeat of these courses will not, however, affect the normal sequence of students’ selection of courses. Students who cannot meet the Grade “C-” requirement in any one of the courses mentioned above after two attempts will be required to withdraw from the University.
