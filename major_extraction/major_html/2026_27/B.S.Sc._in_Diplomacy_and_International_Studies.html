(f)

Exchange Studies:

9

 

 

Students are required to study outside Hong Kong for one academic term in a partner institution via an exchange programme administered by the Programme, the Office of Academic Links or the Colleges, and complete at least 9 units of courses related to Diplomacy/International Relations/Law approved by the Programme Director. Grades obtained in these courses are not included in the calculation of GPA. Students who are unable to obtain the required units from partner institutions must make up the shortfall by completing appropriate courses at CUHK, subject to the approval of the Programme Director.
