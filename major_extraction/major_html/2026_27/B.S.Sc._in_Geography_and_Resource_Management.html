Concentration Area[b]:

Students may choose to declare one, or up to two concentration areas by taking at least 12 units in the relevant area(s):

(a)

Geo-spatial Data Science

 

Any four courses from the following, with at least one course from GRMD2104 or 3106:

GRMD2104, 2106, 3104, 3106, 3108, 3109, 4101, 4403, 4502, 5110

(b)

Physical Environment and Resource Management

 

Any four courses from the following, with at least one course from GRMD3202 or 3205:

GRMD2209, 2221, 2292, 2402, 2403, 3202, 3203, 3205, 3209, 3224, 3401, 3404, 4202, 4203, 4204, 4403

(c)

Urban and Regional Development

 

Any four courses from the following, with at least one course from GRMD2321, 3305 or 3323:

GRMD2303, 2321, 3302, 3304, 3305, 3323, 3402, 4302

(d)

Smart Cities and Sustainability Governance

 

GRMD2501, and at least three courses from the following:

GRMD2401, 3106, 3203, 3305, 3323, 4502, 4503

(e)

Environmental Science and Management

 

GRMD2232, 2401, 3231, 3232, and at least one course from the following:

GRMD3202, 3401

 

Explanatory Notes:

1.

GRMD courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

 

[b]

The five courses should not include courses that have been used to fulfill the requirements in 2(a) to (c).

The courses to be used to fulfill the requirements of concentration areas should not include courses that have been used to fulfill the requirements in 2(a) to (c).
