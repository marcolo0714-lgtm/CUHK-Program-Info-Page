Students admitted to the Mathematics Programme through the broad-based admission scheme are automatically placed in Mathematics Stream. A student who plans to graduate in any other stream(s) may select the relevant courses as prescribed below, and apply at the final year of attendance to specialize in the stream(s) concerned. To specialize in the Enrichment Stream at their final year of attendance, such a student must also have obtained a Cumulative Major GPA of 3.3 or above, or the preceding two semesters’ Major GPA of 3.3 or above. Students admitted to the Enrichment Mathematics Programme are automatically placed in the Enrichment Stream; so no declaration is required.
