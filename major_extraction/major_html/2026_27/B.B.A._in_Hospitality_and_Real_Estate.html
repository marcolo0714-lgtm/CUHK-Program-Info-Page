Major Programme Requirement (for Associate Degree or Higher Diploma holders admitted to senior-year places)

 

Students are required to complete a minimum of 52 units of courses as follows:

 

 

Units

1.

Required Courses:

28

 

FINA2010#, HTMG2000, 2070, 3010, 3030, 3041, 3091, 3092, 3900, 4091, 4092, 4800, 4900 (capstone course), MKTG2010#

 

 

 

 

2.

Stream Courses:

15

 

Students are required to choose one of the following streams:

 

(a)

Hospitality

 

 

HTMG3020, 3060, 3521, 3527, 4600 (with a topic in Hospitality)

 

 

To further strengthen the stream area, students are recommended to take MGNT4050, 4060, 4140, 4540, MKTG3020, 3030, 4040, 4080 for free electives.

 

(b)

Real Estate

 

 

HTMG3502, 3523, 4100, 4190, 4600 (with a topic in Real Estate)

 

 

To further strengthen the concentration area, students are recommended to take ECON3440, FINA3010, 3060, 3080, GRMD2105 for free electives.
