Explanatory Notes:

1.

ACCT courses at 2000 and above level (except those marked with *) as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification if students specialize in the respective streams.

2.

Subject to the approval of the Chairpersons/Directors of the Departments/Schools offering the courses, students may be exempted from up to 3 required courses at 1000 or 2000 level but NOT the units on the strength of the courses completed in AD programmes. No course exemption will be granted to courses at 3000 and above level. If exemption is granted to some of the required courses, students are required, at the full discretion of the Director of the School of Accountancy, to complete other major electives or courses at 3000 and above level offered by the Faculty of Business Administration so as to fulfill the unit requirement of the programme.
