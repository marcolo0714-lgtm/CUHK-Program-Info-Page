Explanatory Notes:

1.

ANTH courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Up to three related courses (9 units) offered by other Departments or Programmes (undergraduate or postgraduate) as approved by this Department may be recognized as courses for fulfillment of major programme requirement.

[a]

Subject to the Department’s prior approval, students are allowed to take both ANTH4300 and 4301 in the same term in their final year of study after finishing a one-year or one-term exchange programme.

Voluntary Concentrations:

Students are encouraged to choose one, or up to two concentrations by taking the relevant courses in fulfillment of the Major Programme requirement listed above. Students will be required to complete a minimum of five courses designated to the concentration of their choice.
