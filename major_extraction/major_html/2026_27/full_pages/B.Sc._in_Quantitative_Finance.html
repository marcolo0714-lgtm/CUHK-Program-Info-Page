Explanatory Notes:

1.

FINA courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled with # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Non-JUPAS admittees and JUPAS admittees without HKDSE Mathematics Extended Modules I or II are required to take MATH1530 before taking MATH1010 or 1018, except those who pass the Mathematics Placement Test arranged by the Programme.

3.

Completion of an eligible second major or minor programme from the Faculty of Engineering (Computer Science, Electronic Engineering, Information Engineering) or the Faculty of Science (Mathematics, Physics, Risk Management Science, Statistics) is deemed to have satisfied 4(a) Quantitative Methods area of elective courses requirement. Only relevant courses listed under 4(a) will be included in the calculation of Major GPA.

Students are advised to take MGNT2512 once their Global Experiential Learning Activities are completed.

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
Quantitative Finance

1.          Major Programme:

Upon completion of the Programme, students will have:

(1)   Finance and Economics:

- Knowledge of financial instruments, their design, properties and pricing, the functions and operation of the financial markets as well as the regulatory environment

- Financial economic theories and their applications 

(2)   Quantitative Methods:

- Mathematical models employed in financial theories and applications of quantitative skills to the design and pricing of sophisticated financial instruments

- Knowledge of statistical methods used in financial theories and their applications

(3)   Information Engineering:

- Knowledge of information technologies used by financial professionals

- Knowledge of the financial market information environment and the skills to retrieve and analyze financial data

(4)   Teamwork and Communication Skills:

- Communication and presentation skills

- Interpersonal skills and the appreciation of the importance of teamwork

(5)   Values/attitudes:

- A global mindset and the curiosity to explore the outside world

- An appreciation of the virtues of integrity, honesty and sense of responsibility

 K = Knowledge outcomes S = Skills outcomes V = Values and attitudes outcomes
