Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
IFAA Outcomes

 

1.          Major Programme:

The graduates from this programme will have 

 

  1. effective business communication skills. (S) 
  2. effective analytical and problem solving skills which support them to make business decisions. (S)
  3. the ability to integrate business knowledge in a business environment. (K/S)
  4. the ability to propose strategies in a global environment. (S)
  5. the ability to be aware of the ethical issues in a business context. (V)
  6. a specific knowledge in insurance, finance and actuarial science. (K/S) 

 

K = Knowledge outcomes S = Skills outcomes V = Values and attitudes outcomes
