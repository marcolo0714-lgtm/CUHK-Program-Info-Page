Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

 

1.      Major Programme:

 

Knowledge

  • Students will have a comprehensive understanding of the various theories and concepts of geography and resource management.
  • Students will have specialized knowledge of a specific area of concentration in the field of geography and resource management and developed a capacity to theorize and generalize knowledge in their area of concentration.
  • Students will have developed a life-long ability to analyze, explain and reflect on real-world spatial development and resource management issues covered in the curriculum.
  • Students will have developed a capacity to apply various theories and methods of geography and resource management in their further studies and career development.

Skills

  • Students will have the ability to apply various quantitative and qualitative geographical analysis and investigation techniques to find solutions for practical problems.
  • Students will have developed essential generic skills such as critical thinking, problem solving and self-managed learning etc.
  • Students will have possessed communication, interpersonal and leadership skills that they can apply in real-world contexts.

Values

  • Students will have embraced the perspective of sustainable development and become sensitive to their responsibilities with reference to spatial development and resource management issues.
  • Students will have developed a capacity to compare, contrast and respect the social, economic and environmental values of places at different geographical scales and the significance of local and regional culture in the context of globalization.

 

2.      Minor Programme:

(i)

Geography and Resource Management

 

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the theories and methods of geography and resource management, acquired generic analytical and problem solving skills and developed sensitivity towards sustainable development issues with sufficient depth and at a suitable level to allow further study in geography and resource management subjects or to pursue a related career.

 

(ii)

Geo-spatial Science for Sustainability

 

Upon completion of the Minor Programme, students will be able to:

 

1.

Master the core concepts of GIS, remote sensing, spatial analytics, and sustainability; (K)

 

2.

Gain knowledge of how geo-spatial science can inform decision-making for sustainable development; (K)

 

3.

Understand how to apply spatial data and analysis to address sustainability issues; (K)

 

4.

Acquire skills in spatial data collection, maintenance and management; (S)

 

5.

Be proficient in using geo-spatial analysis tools and techniques; (S)

 

6.

Be able to address economic, social and environmental sustainability challenges through geo-spatial data analytical methods; (S)

 

7.

Develop the ability to interpret and visualize geo-spatial data effectively; (S)

 

8.

Be able to communicate effectively with stakeholders through mapping and data visualization; (S)

 

9.

Be able to facilitate and converge diverse ideas into solutions that cater to the underlying needs and values; (S/V)

 

10

Cultivate the capacity to work with interdisciplinary teams on sustainability projects; (S/V)

 

11.

Cultivate the capacity for life-long learning and a strong sense of commitment to embodying the values of Diversity, Equity and Inclusion in every step of endeavours towards sustainability goals. (V)

 

 

 

 

K = Knowledge outcomes

 

V= Values and attitude outcomes

 

S = Skills outcomes
