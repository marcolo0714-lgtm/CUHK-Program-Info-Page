Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
1

 

1.

Major Programme:

Knowledge outcomes:

(1)

Solid foundation in life science, including model organisms, fundamental biochemistry, cell biology and genetics;

(2)

Core knowledge in methods in biochemistry, molecular biology, genetic engineering, microbial biotechnology, plant biotechnology and animal biotechnology;

(3)

In-depth knowledge in some specific areas, such as bioinformatics, protein biochemistry and proteomics, developmental biology, physiology;

(4)

Hands-on operational ability in basic skills of molecular biotechnology;

(5)

An educated understanding on the business and social implications of biotechnology, such as government policy, management, intellectual property, and ethical and public concerns.

 

 

Skills outcomes:

(1)

Skills in logical and critical thinking;

(2)

Analytical skills (qualitative and quantitative) and problem-solving ability;

(3)

Ability in oral and written communication;

(4)

Competence in using information technologies and basic computer skills.

 

 

Values and attribute outcomes:

(1)

A positive attitude toward life science, especially molecular biotechnology;

(2)

An educated mind and responsible attitude to judge the pros and cons of various applications of molecular biotechnology on human society and natural environment.

 

 

2.

Minor Programme:

Upon the completion of the Minor Programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career.
