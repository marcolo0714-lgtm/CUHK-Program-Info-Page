Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
ELEDN_Learning Outcomes_ENG

 

1.

Major Programme:

Graduates from the programme will be able to:

(1)

nurture professional English teachers with conceptual and theoretical knowledge in subject matter, curricular and pedagogy; (K/S/V)

(2)

develop reflective English teachers with a comprehensive view and insight on educational issues in the local and global contexts; (K/S/V) and

(3)

produce effective English teachers with solid and strong application skills for communication, teaching and research. (K/S/V)

 

K = Knowledge outcomes; S = Skills outcomes; V = Values & attitude outcomes
