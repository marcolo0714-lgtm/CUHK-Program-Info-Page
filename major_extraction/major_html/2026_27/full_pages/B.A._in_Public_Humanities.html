Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
PUHSN_Learning Outcomes

1.

Major programme:

 

Upon completion of this Major Programme, graduates will have acquired the following learning outcomes in three aspects:

 

 

 

Knowledge outcomes:

(1)

Different forms of cultural practices, expressions, and meanings

 

(2)

Meanings of collective identity, taste, and values

 

(3)

The economic aspects of cultural production, circulation, and consumption

 

(4)

Community preservation and regeneration

 

(5)

Importance of cultural diversity and cultural participation

 

(6)

Art history with a focus on the arts of Hong Kong, China, and Asia

 

(7)

Cultural history and heritage of Hong Kong, China, and Asia

 

(8)

Basic knowledge of proposal writing and arts festivals programming

 

(9)

Basic knowledge in digital humanities

 

(10)

Basic knowledge in public humanities

 

(11)

Basic knowledge in arts tech

 

 

Skills outcomes:

(1)

Arts appreciation

 

(2)

Organization and management skills in working with different parties

 

(3)

Critical skills in cultural interpretation

 

(4)

Basic media skills, writing skills and design skills

 

(5)

Curatorial planning

 

(6)

Research skills to develop cultural policy

 

(7)

Interviewing skills

 

(8)

Development of oral history

 

(9)

Analysis of non-tangible cultural heritage

 

 

Values and attitude outcomes:

(1)

Importance of diversity and pluralism in society

 

(2)

Intercultural sensitivity and respect

 

(3)

A balance of local, national, and global perspectives

 

(4)

Critical awareness related to issues pertaining to gender, racial, class, and national identities

 

(5)

Literacy in different forms of visual and verbal expressions, understanding how meanings and affects are constructed in different media forms and genres

 

(6)

Ability to draw on a number of disciplinary origins within areas of humanities and social sciences to appreciate the richness and the usefulness of interdisciplinary approaches

 

(7)

Commitment to developing a more inclusive and caring society

 

 

 

 

2

Minor programme:

 

1.

Cultural Studies

Upon the completion of the Minor programme, students will have demonstrated basic understanding of fundamental issues related to the subject, acquired knowledge of some aspects of the subject with sufficient exposure and at a suitable level to allow further study in this subject or related subjects or to pursue a related career

 

 

 

 

2.

Film and Screen Studies

 

Upon completion of the programme, students will have acquired the following learning outcomes in three aspects:

 

 

 

 

Knowledge outcomes:

 

(1)

Demonstrate a critical appreciation of some of the dominant concepts, methods, and debates informing the study of film and screen.

 

(2)

Demonstrate a capacity to analyze the form and content of particular audio-visual products.

 

(3)

Demonstrate an awareness of the variety of ways in which audio-visual products and aspects of individual and collective social lives are mutually constituted.

 

(4)

Demonstrate an understanding of different traditions of film and screen practices in different national and international contexts.

 

 

 

 

Skills outcomes:

 

(1)

Demonstrate skills in the close formal and structural analysis of different kinds of audio- visual products.

 

(2)

Demonstrate skills in the research and evaluation of relevant critical and historical materials for the study of film and screen.

 

(3)

Demonstrate an ability to interrelate texts and discourses specific to their own discipline with issues in the wider context of cultural and intellectual history.

 

(4)

Demonstrate an ability to understand and analyze relevant theoretical ideas and to apply these ideas to audio-visual forms.

 

 

 

 

Attitude outcomes:

 

(1)

Through seminar work and group presentations, demonstrate communication skills and an ability to work both individually and in groups.

 

(2)

Acknowledge the contested and provisional nature of knowledge and understanding and show openness towards diverse positions and respect for differing views.
