Explanatory Notes:

1.

Students enrolled in the Mathematical Sciences and Artificial Intelligence Double Major Programme should normally spend four regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study, except otherwise specified in 2. below.

2.

Students who join term-time exchange programme must spend at least three regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study.

3.

Students enrolled in the Mathematical Sciences and Artificial Intelligence Double Major Programme can take substitute/equivalent/alternative courses of University Core Requirements either at CUHK or CUHK(SZ). The list of substitute/equivalent/alternative University Core Requirements courses is available at this link (https://www.zsims.cuhk.edu.hk/en/). Students should not take a course and its substitute/equivalent/alternative course. Should they do so, only one course will be counted towards the fulfillment of the unit requirements. Grades of relevant substitute/equivalent/alternative courses taken at CUHK(SZ) will be transferred to CUHK for the purpose of calculation of GPA, Major GPA and credit units.

4.

All Faculty Package, courses at 2000 or above level in the requirements of the 1st Major Programme, as well as those labeled as #, and the Research Component/Capstone Course/Final Year Project, will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

5.

Courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the 1st Major cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of requirements of the 2nd Major, and vice versa.

6.

A student who wishes to transfer to the programmes offered by the Faculty of Science or Faculty of Engineering shall follow the prevailing rules and regulations of the University and obtain prior permission from the School Director through written application. Such applications should be made one month before the commencement of the University’s designated application period for change of programme. No such application shall be accepted once the application period as announced by the University has commenced. In general, students must apply for a change of programme no later than the third year of attendance of their undergraduate studies.

7.

Students admitted by CUHK will be conferred a Bachelor of Science degree in Mathematical Sciences and Artificial Intelligence upon completion of all graduation requirements. Students may apply to pursue Ph.D. study under the arrangements of the Articulated B.Sc.–Ph.D. Programme (ABP) in Mathematical Sciences, and they must declare the opt-in according to the stipulated procedures by the beginning of the fourth year of attendance.  Students are required to obtain a Bachelor of Science degree with honours not lower than Second Class and to satisfy the minimum admission requirements prescribed by the Graduate School and Zhizhen School in order to be eligible to proceed with postgraduate studies, subject to the prevailing University regulations and approval by the relevant authorities. Graduates from the Bachelor’s programmes from CUHK are deemed to have fulfilled the University’s English Language requirements.

8.

Students who wish to pursue Ph.D. study under the ABP should take note of the following:

 

(i)

For CUHK students who are subsequently admitted to the Ph.D. of the ABP, the 5000-level courses that they complete under this programme will be automatically recognized to fulfill the coursework requirement for the Ph.D. Programme, subject to prevailing guidelines.  No application for course and unit exemptions is required.

 

(ii)

In order to fulfill the graduation requirements for the postgraduate study, students must complete a minimum of 9 months of non-local learning/research experience, with at least 3 consecutive months of such experience. A formal term-time exchange programme during undergraduate study, or non-consecutive and substantial non-local learning/research experiences of 4 months or more offered by the University, Colleges, the Faculty of Engineering or Science, Zhizhen School, the Mathematics Department and other relevant programmes may also be considered, subject to the approval of the Division Head.

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 

Major Programme:

Upon completion of the Programme,

 

Knowledge and Comprehension

Students will have acquired the following knowledge:

(1)

Linear Algebra and Abstract Algebra;

(2)

Analysis which includes Calculus, Real Analysis, Complex Analysis, Fourier Analysis and Functional Analysis;

(3)

Differential Geometry;

(4)

Computer programming for Mathematics and Numerical Analysis;

(5)

Theoretical and practical aspects of artificial intelligence.

 

 

Skills and Generic Competencies

Students will have acquired a set of generic competencies:

(1)

Ability to understand broad mathematical literature independently;

(2)

Awareness of mathematical problems arising in generic situations;

(3)

An ability to communicate mathematical problems effectively;

(4)

Ability to formulate mathematical problems and know how to find ways to solve them;

(5)

Ability to implement AI/machine learning methods with modern computing tools;

(6)

Ability to evaluate AI/machine learning models using appropriate metrics;

(7)

Ability to solve real-life problems using mathematical software and AI/machine learning.

 

 

Professional Skills

In addition, students will have possessed professional skills in at least one of the following sectors:

(1)

Graduate school research;

(2)

Teaching;

(3)

Interdisciplinary research between mathematics and other scientific disciplines.

 

 

Value and attitudes

Upon graduation, our students will be able to apply their mathematical knowledge generically in their future profession in a way that is unique for mathematically trained experts. They will be able to make important original contributions in mathematical sciences and lead breakthrough of interdisciplinary mathematical-science applications.
