1.

AIST, BMED, CHEM, CSCI, DOTE, DSPS, ECON, EDUC, EESC, IERG, LSCI, MATH, PHYS, RMSC and STAT courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major Programme Requirement will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

Subject to approval of the Programme Director, BMED4910 and 4920 can be used to substitute two of the required courses under Pedagogy.

Capstone courses

[c]

[d]

[e]

[f]

Students can take all DSPS courses except DSPS3801, 4801, 4802.

[g]

Students can take all AIST courses at 2000 or above level.

[h]

Students can take all IERG courses at 4000 or below level.
