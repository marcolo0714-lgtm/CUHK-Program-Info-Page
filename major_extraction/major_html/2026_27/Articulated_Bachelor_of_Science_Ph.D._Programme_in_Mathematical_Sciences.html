Explanatory Notes:

1.

All Faculty Package courses, Required Courses, and Elective Courses will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Courses at 5000 and 6000 level completed in undergraduate study will be automatically recognised to fulfill the Coursework Requirement for the Ph.D. Programme. No application for course and unit exemptions is required.

3.

Students will be conferred the Bachelor’s degree, subject to the fulfillment of the undergraduate graduation requirements.

4.

To be eligible to proceed with the postgraduate studies, students are required to satisfy the minimum admission requirements prescribed by the Graduate School and Zhizhen School after completion of the fourth year.

5.

Students are required to sit for the Candidacy Examination in their fourth year of attendance, even if they have not yet completed their undergraduate studies. They have to pass the Candidacy Examination before the end of the first year of attendance of postgraduate study to satisfy the Candidacy Examination requirement for the Ph.D. Programme. Students may attempt to take the Candidacy Examination in their third year of attendance subject to approval of the School Director. Students who win individual gold or silver awards in the Yau College Student Mathematics Competition or equivalent may apply for exemption from the Candidacy Examination in the corresponding direction.

6.

In order to fulfill the graduation requirements for postgraduate study, students must complete a minimum of 9 months of non-local learning/ research experience, with at least 3 consecutive months of such experience. A formal term-time exchange programme during undergraduate study, or non-consecutive and substantial non-local learning/ research experiences of 4 months or more offered by the University, Colleges, the Faculty of Engineering or Science, Zhizhen School, the Mathematics Department and other relevant programmes may also be considered, subject to the approval of the Division Head.

7.

A student who wishes to transfer to the programmes offered by the Faculty of Science or Faculty of Engineering, or relevant programmes of other Faculties shall follow the prevailing rules and regulations of the University and obtain prior permission from the School Director through written application. Such applications should be made one month before the commencement of designated application period for change of programme. No such application shall be accepted once the application period has commenced. In general, students must apply for a change of programme no later than the third year of attendance of their undergraduate studies.

[a]

Either one of the two courses taken to fulfill the Required Courses requirement cannot be used to fulfill the Elective Courses requirement.

 

 

Doctor of Philosophy in Mathematical Sciences

 

 

 

Pre-candidacy

 

 

The “candidacy requirement” composes of four major parts, namely coursework requirement, candidacy examination, thesis proposal (and oral defence) and stipulated Improving Postgraduate Learning (IPL) module requirements. Students must complete and fulfill the four parts within the maximum period stipulated by the Programme. Details of the requirement are listed below:

 

Units

1.

Coursework Requirement

 

(a)

Lecture courses:

15

 

15 units of MATH or IMSC courses at 5000 and 6000 level, with at least 9 units of these courses covering at least three of the following directions:

 

(i)

Algebra and Number Theory

 

 

IMSC5011, 5012, MATH5051, 5052, 6031, 6032, 6061, 6062

 

(ii)

Analysis and Partial Differential Equations

 

 

IMSC5021, 5022, MATH5011, 5012, 5021, 5022, 6041, 6042, 6081, 6082

 

(iii)

Computational and Applied Mathematics

 

 

IMSC5041, 5042, MATH5211, 5212, 6211, 6212, 6221, 6222, 6231, 6232, 6241, 6242, 6251, 6261

 

(iv)

Geometry and Topology

 

 

IMSC5031, 5032, MATH5070, 5031, 5032, 5061, 5062, 6021, 6022, 6051, 6052, 6071, 6072

 

(b)

Thesis research/ monitoring courses:

12

 

Students must register for a Thesis Research course every term: MATH8006

 

 

 

 

2.

Candidacy Examination

 

 

Students need to pass the Candidacy Examination (written and oral examinations) in 3 directions out of 4. The Candidacy Examination directions consist of: (a) Algebra and Number Theory, (b) Analysis and Partial Differential Equations, (c) Computational and Applied Mathematics, and (d) Geometry and Topology.

Those who win individual gold or silver awards in the Yau College Student Mathematics Competition or equivalent are allowed to apply for exemption from the Candidacy Examination in the corresponding direction.

 

 

 

 

3.

Thesis Proposal and Oral Defence

 

 

Students must submit a research proposal endorsed by the supervisor, and pass an oral defence.

 

 

 

 

4.

Improving Postgraduate Learning (IPL) modules:

 

 

Students are required to complete and pass the following Improving Postgraduate Learning (IPL) modules in their first year of attendance for partial fulfilment of candidacy requirements:

 

(a)

An IPL module on “Basics of Research Data Management”

This is an online module and relevant information can be accessed from the website: https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/clear/download/IPL-Researchskills.pdf.

 

(b)

Students are required to submit a Data Management Plan (DMP), with effect from the 2025-26 intake.

 

 

 

 

5.

Other Requirements

 

(a)

Students must fulfill the Term Assessment Requirement of the Graduate School. For details, please refer to Clause 13.0 “Unsatisfactory Performance and Discontinuation of Studies” of the General Regulations Governing Postgraduate Studies which can be accessed from the Graduate School Homepage: https://www.gs.cuhk.edu.hk.

3.

Improving Postgraduate Learning (IPL) modules:

 

 

Students are required to complete the following IPL modules for graduation:

 

(a)

An IPL module on “Observing Intellectual Property and Copyright Law during Research”. This is an online module and relevant information can be accessed from the website:

https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/clear/prodev/ipl.html.

 

(b)

An IPL module on “Becoming an Excellent TA: Professional Development Course for Teaching Assistants”.

 

(c)

Students must complete an online Research Ethics Training (RET) module on “Publication Ethics” offered by the Office of Research and Knowledge Transfer Services (ORKTS) and obtain a valid Publication Ethics Certificate for graduation.  Relevant information can be accessed from the RET website at https://www.research-ethics.cuhk.edu.hk/web/.

 

(d)

The final DMP should have been shared to the CUHK community on

DMP Tool or the relevant platform that CUHK provides when submitting the final version of the thesis/portfolio to the Graduate School. Students are required to have published the data on the CUHK Research Data Repository or an open data repository with a valid Digital Object Identifier (DOI) or other forms of permanent identifier when submitting the final version of the thesis/portfolio. Upon thesis supervisor’s endorsement, students can decide on how much data from the research project is to be published.

(b)

Students are required to submit a research thesis and pass an oral defence for graduation.
