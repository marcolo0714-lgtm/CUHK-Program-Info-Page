Explanatory Notes:

1.

PSYC courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Subject to approval by the Major Programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major Programme only. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the 1st Major Programme as a single major. Students should be aware that such decision may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.

[a]

Students are required to take PSYC2010. Psychology & Journalism and Communication Double Major Programme students who have taken any one of the equivalent statistics courses including DOTE2011, ECON2121, GRMD2102, LING3403, PHPC2015, SEEM2430, SOCI2004, SPED2600, STAT2001 and 2006, STAT2101 and 2102 are exempted from taking PSYC2010, and they are required to take another PSYC course listed under 3(a) Elective Courses (2000 or above level) to compensate for the units.

[b]

Students are required to take PSYC3430. Psychology & Journalism and Communication Double Major Programme students who have taken ENGE3610 or LING3202 will be exempted from taking PSYC3430, and they are required to take another PSYC course listed under 3(a) Elective Courses (2000 or above level) to compensate for the units.
