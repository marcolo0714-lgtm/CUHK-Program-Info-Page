Overseas Experience [b]: Students are required to complete at least one of the following: (1) a regular term-time academic exchange (organized by OAL or the Colleges); (2) a summer/winter study abroad programme. Students should complete 3 units of business-related courses from (1) or (2) with prior approval from the major programme. Academic results obtained will not be included in the calculation of the Major GPA. Under special circumstances, students who are unable to fulfil this requirement, must make up for the shortfall in units by completing appropriate courses at CUHK, subject to the approval of the major programme.

Explanatory Notes:

 

1.

GLEF courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled with # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

 

2.

Double majors in GLEF and ECON, GLEF and IFAA, and GLEF and QFIN are not allowed.

 

3.

Students of GLEF Programme who wish to claim IBBA as second major must choose a concentration area other than the Finance concentration area.
