Major Programme Requirement

 

 

Students are required to complete a minimum of 63 units of courses as follows:

 

 

Units

1.

Faculty Package:

9

 

PHIL1110* and any two courses from:

ANTH1020, ARTS1005, 1009, 1010, BMBL1001, CHES1101 or 1102, CHLL1900 or 1902 or 1903, CURE1000 or 1400, 1110, ENGE1000, FAAS1900, HIST1000 or 1700 or 1701, JASP1090, LING1000, MUSC1000, THEO1000, TRAN1000

 

 

2.

Required Courses:

30

 

PHIL1110*, 1310, 2020, 2030, 2040, 2050, 2060, 3000, 3800, 3820, 4800 (Capstone course)

 

 

3.

Elective Courses:

24

 

All Philosophy courses other than PHIL1110 and the 30 units of required courses listed in (2) are elective courses. Students are required to take at least 6 units each from Areas 1, 2 and 3. Final year students are allowed to take a maximum of 6 units of courses from Area 5.

 

 

Total:

63

* PHIL1110 counted as Faculty package units only.

 

Explanatory Notes:

1.

PHIL courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students should obtain Grade “C-” or above in each of the courses of PHIL1110 and 1310. Otherwise, they are required to repeat the course(s). The repeat of the course(s) will not, however, affect the normal sequence of students’ selection of courses. Students who cannot meet the Grade “C-” requirement in any one of the courses mentioned above after two attempts will be required to withdraw from the University.

3.

Exemption of units may be granted to students given permission to take two minor programmes. The Department will consider each application on a case-by-case basis.
