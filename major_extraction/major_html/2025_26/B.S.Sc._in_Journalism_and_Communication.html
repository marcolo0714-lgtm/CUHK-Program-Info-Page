Explanatory Notes:

1.

COMM courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

3.

Students may take COMM courses outside of their study schemes as their Major elective courses, and the course grades will be included in the calculation of Major GPA.
