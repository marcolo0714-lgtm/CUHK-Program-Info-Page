Students are required to study at two overseas institutions in Europe and North America through student exchange or study abroad programmes administered by the Faculty of Business Administration, Office of Academic Links or the Colleges for one academic term respectively, and complete 6 units of business-related courses in each institution approved by the Programme Director. Academic results obtained from the overseas institutions will not be included in the calculation of the Major GPA for honours classification. Under special circumstances, students who are unable to study at or complete the required number of units of courses at the overseas institutions must make up the shortfall by completing appropriate courses subject to the approval of Programme Director.

ACCT/DOTE/FINA/IBBA/MGNT/MKTG courses at 2000 and above level (excluding ACCT2111, 2121, IBBA3040, MGNT2511 and 2512) will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.
