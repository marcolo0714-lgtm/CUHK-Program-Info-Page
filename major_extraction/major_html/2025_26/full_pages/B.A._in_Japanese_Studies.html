Students who passed placement test and exempted from taking any or all required language course(s) (JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and/or 2480) are required to take corresponding units of Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level specified under 3(b) above, to substitute for the relevant course(s). These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

 

(ii)

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of Elective Courses specified under 3 above, with at least 12 units of Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

 

(iii)

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N1) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of Elective Courses specified under 3 above, with at least 9 units of Language courses at 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

 

(iv)

Native Japanese students are required to take 18 units of Elective Courses specified under 3 above, with or without any Language courses, to substitute for the four relevant courses. If they choose to take Language courses, they are allowed to take Language courses at 4000 level only, subject to the approval of the course teacher. If they choose to take non-language courses, they should take at least 12 units of elective courses at 2000 or above level.

 

Approved courses taken in substitution for JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480 will be included in the Major GPA calculation.

[c]

Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in relation to the resident study in Japan.

[d]   

These courses are only offered to students before their resident study in Japan.

Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
JASP

1.

Major Programme:

 

Upon completing the Programme, students will have:

(1)

attained the ability to think critically and express their views in oral and written form using well constructed arguments and by presenting evidence;

(2)

demonstrated a broad understanding of Japanese society and culture in domestic, regional and global contexts;

(3)

demonstrated a mastery of a special topic within Japanese studies based on original data collection and a comprehensive survey of relevant academic literature;

(4)

attained the ability to plan and execute a complex, long-term research project;

(5)

attained the ability to draw upon multiple disciplinary perspectives and approaches to analyze Japan and their own society;

(6)

attained the ability to identify and analyze Japanese-language resources for research purposes;

(7)

attained the ability to communicate effectively using Japanese language in the appropriate social and cultural contexts;

(8)

attained the ability to use Japanese language at both concrete and abstract levels to express ideas and opinions;

(9)

developed inter-cultural sensitivity on personal and intellectual levels; and

(10)

developed the skills and attitudes that facilitate life-long learning.

 

 

2.

Japanese Studies and History Double Major Programme:

 

Upon completing the Programme, students will have acquired:

(1)

command of Japanese language; (K)

(2)

progress in humanities research, particularly historical research and Japanese Studies; (K)

(3)

deepened understanding of both Japanese and Chinese culture and history; (K)

(4)

critical and creative thinking; (S)  

(5)

communication skills and computer literacy; (S)  

(6)

self-managed learning, adaptability and problem solving skills; (S)  

(7)

ability to appreciate East Asian cultural diversity, exchanges and mutual dependence; (V)  

(8)

ability to value and appreciate the beauty of language and culture and the joy of humanities research; and (V) 

(9)

ability to help consolidate cosmopolitan identity and cultural openness of East Asia. (V)  

 

 

K = Knowledge outcomes        S = Skills outcomes         V = Values and attitude outcomes

 

 

3.

Minor Programme:

 

(i)            

Japanese Language

(ii)          

Japanese Studies
