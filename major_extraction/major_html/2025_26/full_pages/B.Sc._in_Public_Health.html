Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 

1.      Major Programme

Upon completing the programme, students will be able to:

 

Knowledge domain

1.             Acquire the contemporary concepts of public health and the interactions between determinants of health at individual, local and global levels.

2.             Explain from global perspective the burden of disease, the links between health and development, and approaches to international cooperation to monitor, promote and protect health.

3.             Describe the important issues involved in public health advocacy, including ethical reasoning, civic engagement, and social justice.

 

Skill domain

1.             Assesse the health status, social concern, needs and equity of different populations contributing to the use of health services and health promotion practices.

2.             Design framework for primary, secondary and tertiary care and prevention.

3.             Utilize evidence based approach to inquire, analyze and criticize contemporary public health issues.

 

Value domain

1.             Demonstrate the appreciation of the importance of primary care in population health.

2.             Acknowledge the cross-cultural impacts on actual and perceived health needs.

3.             Uphold the core value of social equity and justice.
