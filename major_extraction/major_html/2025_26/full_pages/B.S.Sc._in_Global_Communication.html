Study Scheme
Learning Outcomes
 
Global Communication

 

1.          Major Programme:

 

I. Knowledge outcomes

(1) A solid theoretical and practical basis for studying and analyzing global communication phenomena

(2) Knowledge of theoretical and practical skills and the ability to apply those skills to convey informational and/or creative messages and address social problems for the common good in local and international contexts

(3) Specialized spectrum of knowledge characteristic of the professional areas of journalism, creative and new media, and communication studies

 

II. Skill outcomes

(A) Generic skill outcomes

(1) Problem solving skills and analytical skills to be utilized in examining issues in transnational and crosscultural communications

(2) Critical thinking, and especially an ability to reflect on issues of cultural diversity

(3) Interpersonal, social and leadership skills, especially in the contexts of intercultural communication and transnational organizations

 

(B) Specific skill outcomes

(1) Multimedia production and presentation skills, which would allow the students to be competent content producers in the global communication arena

(2) Professional and creative writing skills

(3) An ability to unearth, collect, decipher, and integrate information in the global and digital media environment

 

III. Value outcomes

(1) Professional ethics conducive to communicating messages in a truthful, accurate, and fair manner

(2) Sociocultural awareness of and concern for contemporary social and global media issues

(3) Commitment and passion towards professional development, intellectual curiosity and life-long learning

 

2.          Minor Programme:

 

Upon completion of this Minor Programme, students will have acquired:

(1)   Conceptual understanding of media and communication intersections (Knowledge). Students will gain a comprehensive understanding of how media and communication intersect with politics, economy, culture, and human flow at a global scale. This includes the ability to analyse and interpret the ways in which media discourse is shaped by political and economic factors, and how they, in turn, influence political and economic outcomes. Students will also learn about the role of media and communication in cultural exchange and understanding, and how media can both reflect and shape cultural norms and values.

 

(2)   Ability to communicate with global audiences (Skills). The Programme equips students with the skills to effectively communicate with global audiences through different media forms. This includes the ability to write clear, compelling, and contextually appropriate content for diverse international audiences.

 

(3)   Appreciation, sensibility, and criticality of media outputs and communication practices (Values). Key value outcomes of the Programme include the development of appreciation and sensibility regarding the societal impacts of one’s own media outputs and communication practices, as well as the criticality of the way in which one’s own media outputs impact society. Students will learn to reflect on how their work in media and communication can have far-reaching effects at a global level, influencing public opinion, shaping cultural narratives, and potentially affecting political and economic outcomes.
