Minor Programme Title

Mathematics

 

 

Minor Programme Requirement

 

 

Students are required to complete a minimum of 18 units of courses, with at least 6 units at 3000 or above level, as follows:

 

 

Units

1.

Elective Courses:

18

 

18 units of MATH courses, out of which at least 6 units of courses are at 3000 to 5000 level

 

 

 

 

 

Total:

18

Explanatory Notes:

 

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Mathematics, Mathematics and Information Engineering, Mathematics and Mathematics Education and Interdisciplinary Data Analytics & X Double Major Programme with second Major in Mathematics or Mathematics and Applied Mathematics.

2.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
