Chinese Language and Literature — CUHK Minor Requirements (2024-25)

Arts
Applicable to students admitted in 2024-25.

Description

1. Elective Courses: 21 units

(a) Basic courses
Any 9 units of courses from the following: CHLL1110, 1211, 1401, 2121, 2131, 2212, 2302, 2350, 2403(9 units)
