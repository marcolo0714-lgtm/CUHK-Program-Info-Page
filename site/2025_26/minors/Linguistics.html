Explanatory Notes

1.This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Bimodal Bilingual Studies, and Linguistics, or students who double major in Linguistics and Translation, and Linguistics and Psychology.
2.No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
[a]Including courses listed under 2(a) and 2(b) not taken to fulfill the requirement of 2(a) or 2(b).
