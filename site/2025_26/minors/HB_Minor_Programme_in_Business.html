Explanatory Notes

1.This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Risk Management Science, Global Economics and Finance, Quantitative Finance and Risk Management Science, Bachelor of Business Administration (Integrated BBA Programme) and Juris Doctor Programme, and programmes under the Faculty of Business Administration.
2.This Minor Programme is also not applicable to students who pursue the following programmes: Bachelor of Business Administration in Integrated BBA Programme and Financial Technology Double Major Programme; Bachelor of Engineering in Financial Technology and Integrated BBA Programme Double Major Programme; Bachelor of Science in Biotechnology, Entrepreneurship and Healthcare Management; and Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Data Analytics & X Double Major Programme with 2nd Major in Integrated BBA Programme.
3.No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
4.Students must meet the requirement of a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.7 by the time they declare a Minor in Business.
5.Students should note that there are pre-requisite requirements for some of the courses.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
