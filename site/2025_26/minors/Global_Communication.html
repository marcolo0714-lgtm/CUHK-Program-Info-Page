Explanatory Notes

1.No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
2.Units taken to fulfill the requirement of the Journalism and Communication Programme (including Major Programmes, Double Major Programmes, Double Degree Options and Minor Programme) and Global Studies Programme (including Major Programme and Minor Programme) cannot be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
