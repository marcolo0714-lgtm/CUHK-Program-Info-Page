Explanatory Notes

1.Requirement for declaration in Minors: Students should have obtained a pass or above in COMM1110 for declaration of a Minor in Journalism and Communication.
2.Declaration of a Minor in Journalism and Communication in no way guarantees enrolment in courses with quotas due to limited facilities and manpower.
3.No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
