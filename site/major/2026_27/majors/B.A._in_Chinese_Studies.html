Explanatory Notes

1.

CHES courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major programme requirements will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students are assigned to one of the streams based on language assessment upon admission, subject to the approval of the Programme Director.

3.

Any CHES courses counted towards the fulfillment of one of the above listed requirements cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of any other requirements specified in the study scheme.

4.

All CLCP courses taken to fulfill this Major programme requirement cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the University Core requirements.

[a]

Subject to the approval of the Programme Director, students fluent in Putonghua can fulfill this requirement by taking Cantonese (CLCC) courses.

[b]

Students plan to enroll in CHES3402 must submit applications for approval by a faculty member and the Programme Director.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
