Bachelor of Chinese Medicine — CUHK Major Requirements (2026–27)157 units

Faculty: Medicine · Admission code: BCMEN · 157 units · For students admitted in 2026–27

The Programme consists of two parts: the Pre-clinical Programme (4.5 years) and the Clinical Programme (1.5 years).

Pre-clinical Programme

In the Pre-clinical Programme (4.5 years), students have to complete both required and elective courses including subjects in basic medical and life sciences, Chinese medical concepts and principles of Chinese medicine application, Chinese medicine classics and history, specialties in Chinese medicine and clinical training. Students who take “Clinical Training” courses (i.e. BCME 2401-2405) may need to undergo training in summer vacation.

Clinical Programme

The Clinical Programme extends over 1.5 years. The first year provides students with teaching in clinical settings of clinics and wards in a Chinese medicine hospital in the Mainland. Clinical training will cover various specialties in Chinese medicine including internal medicine, acupuncture and bone setting. Students will rotate among attachments to various clinical departments and write reports on case histories of selected topics. During the last six months, students will receive advanced clinical training in one or two selected specialties by attaching to a Chinese medicine hospital in the Mainland and/ or a specialty clinic in Hong Kong. They will also learn about the operation of Chinese medicine clinics in Hong Kong and the diagnosis and treatment of common medical problems seen in out-patient clinic settings. A professional examination will be arranged for students at the end of each term to assess their academic performance. Students may need to undergo training during summer and winter vacations. About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
