4.6 units

Students are required to study at the University of South Carolina (USC) for one academic year and complete 6 units of designated thematic courses with prior approval from the Faculty of Business Administration before graduation. Units obtained from USC will be recognized as equivalent to up to 18 Major units. Academic results obtained from USC will not be included in the calculation of the Major GPA for honours classification. Under special circumstances, students who are unable to study at or complete the required number of units of courses at the partner institutions must make up the shortfall by completing appropriate courses at CUHK, subject to approval of Programme Director.

5.Internships

Students are required to perform at least one internship during the course of their undergraduate studies. Internship carries no unit but is mandatory requirement for graduation.

Explanatory Notes

1.

ACCT/DOTE/FINA/IBBA/MGNT/MKTG courses at 2000 and above level (excluding ACCT2111, 2121, IBBA3040, MGNT2511 and 2512) will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in relation to the resident study in USC.

3.

Students will not be allowed to declare Management of International Business as their second concentration.

4.

MKTG3010 and the associated units can be used to satisfy the major requirement and one of the second concentrations of the followings: Marketing and Big Data and Quantitative Marketing.

5.

Students claiming Entrepreneurship Concentration are not allowed to declare Minor in Entrepreneurship and Innovation.

[a]

ACCT2151 and 3151 are mutually exclusive. Students who would like to pursue a career in accounting profession are advised to take ACCT3151 instead of ACCT2151.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
