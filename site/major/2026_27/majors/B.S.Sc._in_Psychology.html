Explanatory Notes

1.

PSYC courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

A Psychology Major student who pursues a second Major in Linguistics can be exempted from taking LING3403 and LING3202 by taking PSYC2010 and PSYC3430 respectively. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

[a]

Students are required to take PSYC2010. Students who have transferred to the Psychology Programme and have taken any of the equivalent statistics courses including COMM3110, DOTE2011, ECON2121, GRMD2102, LING3403, PHPC2015, SEEM2430, SOCI2004, SPED2600, STAT2001 and 2006, STAT2101 and 2102 are exempted from taking PSYC2010, and they are required to take another PSYC (2000 or above level) course listed under 3. Elective Courses to compensate for the units.

[b]

Students are required to take PSYC2540. Students who have transferred to the Psychology Programme and have taken SOWK2110 or SOWK2120 or EDUC2310 will be exempted from taking PSYC2540, and they are required to take another PSYC (2000 or above level) course listed under 3. Elective Courses to compensate for the units.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
