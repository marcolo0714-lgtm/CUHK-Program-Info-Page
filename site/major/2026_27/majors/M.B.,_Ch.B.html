M.B., Ch.B. — CUHK Major Requirements (2026–27)

Faculty: Medicine · Admission code: MEDUN · For students admitted in 2026–27

The Medicine Programme is a six-year undergraduate professional programme that aims to prepare students for medical internship and life-long career as medical practitioners. Medical students have to complete the prescribed course of studies and examinations each year before they can be promoted to the following year.

Besides the essential medical knowledge and skills, the curriculum puts equal emphasis on medical professionalism, bioethics and communication skills. These topics are covered through interactive teaching and learning across all years of the programme.

Students learn a wide range of biomedical knowledge and its clinical application in the first three years. They have clinical contact including structured practical sessions in the clinical skills laboratory and clinical rotations to various disciplines.

In the clinical years, students acquire clinical competence through participation in the work of clinical teams in delivering day-to-day care of patients.

Medical students are required to pass all the Professional Examinations and periodic assessments of individual courses and modules for conferment of the MBChB Degrees.

Graduates are required to complete one-year of internship at recognized hospitals before they are qualified to apply for registration as a medical doctor in Hong Kong.

3.MBChB Global Physician-Leadership Stream (GPS)

All GPS students have to complete the study scheme prescribed above and the following courses:

(1)

GPS students admitted in Medicine Year One or Medicine Year Two are required to complete satisfactorily GPS Leadership Workshop Series I (GPSU2000), GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIA (GPSU3000), GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIB (GPSU4000) and GPS Leadership Workshop Series III (GPSU4200).

GPS students admitted in Medicine Year Three are required to complete satisfactorily GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIA (GPSU3000), GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIB (GPSU4000) and GPS Leadership Workshop Series III (GPSU4200).

GPS students admitted in Medicine Year Four are required to complete satisfactorily GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIB (GPSU4000) and GPS Leadership Workshop Series III (GPSU4200). GPS students who fulfill the requirements of GPS Leadership Workshop Series I (GPSU2000) and GPS Leadership Workshop Series IIA (GPSU3000) are eligible for exemption from Medicine Plus: Exploring Contemporary Medicine (MEDU2000).

(2)

Under close supervision of a mentor, GPS students must complete a GPS project within the period from Medicine Year Three or Four until Medicine Year Five. These students are required to submit an end-of-project report no later than the end of Medicine Year Five. GPS students are eligible for exemption from Selected Study Modules (SSMU3000) if they opt for submission of an interim report of the GPS Project by the end of Medicine Year Four.

(3)

GPS students are required to take GPS Internship (a 4 to 6-week duration internship attachment in a local or an overseas institution). GPS Internship is arranged at the latest by the time of Medicine Year Five. GPS students who fulfill the requirement of GPS Internship are eligible for exemption from Clinical Elective (MEDU4410).

Explanatory Notes

1.

Students who have completed all the courses listed below are eligible to apply for exemption of 3 units of University Core English Language Requirement course ELTU2016:

(i)

MEDF1042 Interprofessional Healthcare II;

(ii)

MEDU2500 Doctor and Patient I;

(iii)

MEDU3500 Doctor and Patient II;

(iv)

SSMU3000 Selected Study Modules;

(v)

MEDU4610 Senior Medical Clerkship; and

(vi)

MEDU4710 Senior Surgical Dressership.

2.

Courses taken in bioethics that are offered by the CUHK Centre for Bioethics are considered as alternatives to fulfilling 5 units of the University General Education courses in Areas B, C and D.

3.

Students admitted with qualifications listed below are eligible to apply for exemptions in MEDU1110 Systemic Anatomy (2 units) and MEDU1131 Basic Integrative Pathology (2 units):

(i)

attained a total score of at least 38 in 6 HKDSE subjects, including at least 5* in English, Biology and Chemistry; or

(ii)

admitted to the MBChB Programme on the strength of GCE Advanced Level (AL)/International Advanced Level (IAL) having obtained:

(a)

Grade A* in four AL/IAL subjects with overall average of 95% or above; and

(b)

Grade A* in Biology (AL/IAL) and Chemistry (AL/IAL); or

(iii)

admitted to the MBChB Programme on the strength of IB having obtained:

(a)

total IB score of 43 or above out of 45; and

(b)

at least Grade 6 in Biology (HL) and Chemistry (HL), of which either one must be Grade 7; or

(iv)

admitted to the MBChB Programme on the strength of Cambridge Pre-U having obtained:

(a)

Grade D2 or above in four subjects (which may be substituted by Grade A* in GCE AL/IAL); and

(b)

Grade D2 or above in Biology and Chemistry (which may be substituted by Grade A* in GCE AL/IAL); or

(v)

passed relevant Chemistry and Biology courses at undergraduate degree level, that are approved by the Faculty, encompassing students who:

(a)

have already obtained a bachelor’s (or higher) degree in a science (and related) discipline at a recognized university; or

(b)

have successfully completed a study programme of at least two years’ duration at a medical school whose curriculum is similar and compatible to that of CUHK; or

(c)

have successfully completed a study programme of at least one year’s duration at a medical school whose curriculum is similar and compatible to that of CUHK; or

(d)

are still in their undergraduate studies.

4.

Students are required to apply for the exemptions. When exemption from a particular course is recognized, students can only be exempted from the course but not the units.

5.

The requirements of students with a normative study period different from those prescribed above have to be approved by the Board of the Faculty of Medicine on a case-by-case basis.

6.

Students may opt to take elective course(s) from the course list.
