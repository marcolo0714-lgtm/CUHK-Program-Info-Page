Explanatory Notes

1.

Students enrolled in the Double Major Programme should normally spend four regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study, except otherwise specified in 2. below. It applies to all students, including those who select a 2nd Major offered by CUHK.

2.

Students who join term-time exchange programme have to spend at least three regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study.

3.

Students enrolled in the Double Major programme can take substitute/equivalent/alternative courses either at CUHK or CUHK(SZ). The list of substitute/equivalent/alternative courses is available at this link (https://mase.erg.cuhk.edu.hk/programme-information). Students should not take a course and its substitute/equivalent/alternative course. Should they do so, only one course will be counted towards the fulfillment of units requirements. Grades of relevant substitute/equivalent/alternative courses taken at CUHK(SZ) will be transferred to CUHK for the purpose of calculation of GPA, Major GPA and credit units.

4.

Students normally have to declare their 2nd Major by the end of their first year of study.

5.

All courses at 2000 and above level and courses labeled as # in the requirements of the 1st Major Programme, courses labelled as # in the requirements of the 2nd Major, as well as 3 or 4 units of research component course offered by the 2nd Major, will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

6.

Courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the 1st Major cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of requirements of the 2nd Major, and vice versa.

7.

Under very special circumstances and subject to approval by the Major programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the Materials Science and Engineering Programme offered by CUHK as single major. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the Materials Science and Engineering Programme as a single major and spend the remaining course of study at CUHK. Students should be aware that such decisions may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.
