B.Sc. in Risk Management Science — CUHK Major Requirements (2026–27)72 units

Faculty: Science · Admission code: RMSCN · 72 units · For students admitted in 2026–27

There is one enrichment stream in the Risk Management Science Programme: Risk Analytics Stream. Students who got a total score of 30 or above in the best five HKDSE subjects or have obtained a cumulative GPA of 3.7 or above in the first or second year of attendance may apply for specializing in the Risk Analytics Stream. Students are required to complete a minimum of 72 units of courses as follows:

Explanatory Notes

1.

RMSC courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labelled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students admitted to the Dual Undergraduate Degree Programme (DDP) with The University of Edinburgh (UoE) are required to follow the major programme requirement and will not be able to declare a stream.

[a]

Students may take ECON1010 as a substitute for DOTE1030.

[b]

Students may take RMSC3001 as a substitute for FINA3080.

[c]

Students may take CSCI2100# as a substitute for CSCI2520.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
