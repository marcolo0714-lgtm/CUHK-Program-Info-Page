B.Sc. in Earth and Environmental Sciences — CUHK Major Requirements (2026–27)72 units

Faculty: Science · Admission code: EESCN · 72 units · For students admitted in 2026–27

There are three streams of specialization: 1) Atmospheric Science, 2) Geology and Geophysics, and 3) Environmental Science and Technology. The stream selected by the student will be printed on the transcript. Students admitted to the Earth and Environmental Sciences Major Programme through the broad-based admission scheme may apply at their final year of attendance to specialize in any of the streams and select the relevant courses as prescribed below. Students are required to complete a minimum of 72 units of courses as follows:

Group C: MATH1010 or 1018 or 1520

Group E: STAT1011 or 1012

Two courses from the following [a]

Group A: LSCI1012 (preferred) or 1002

Group B: CHEM1070 (preferred) or 1072

Group D: PHYS1001 or 1002 or 1111 or 1113

Explanatory Notes

1.

BCHE, BIOL, CHEM, CSCI, EEEN, EESC, ELEG, ENGG, ENSC, ESSC, GPAD, GRMD, IERG, LAWS, LSCI, MAEG, MATH, MBTE, PHPC, PHYS, RMSC, STAT, URSP courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

Students are strongly encouraged to take one more course in the remaining group to fulfill part of the Electives requirement in 3(d) in order to broaden their exposure to all areas of science.

[b]

Students in the Atmospheric Science Stream and Geology and Geophysics Stream are strongly encouraged to take courses under CHEM, CSCI, EEEN, ELEG, ENGG, IERG, MAEG, MATH, PHYS, RMSC and STAT to strengthen their quantitative background and partly fulfill this requirement.

[c]

Students may select research-oriented course(s), as approved by the Major Programme, to substitute up to 4 units for fulfillment of Research Courses requirement.

[d]

Students must complete any exchange/research/internship programme(s) offered by the University, Colleges, the Faculty of Science or Major Programme, as approved by the Major Programme, to fulfill the Experiential Learning requirement. Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in the exchange/research/internship programme(s).

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
