Explanatory Notes

1.

SPED and EDUC courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major Programme Requirement as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students who have completed 14 units of Professional Skills Courses will be eligible to apply for exemption of 2 units of University Core Physical Education Requirement. Students are required to apply for the exemption. When exemption from a particular course is recognized, students can only be exempted from the course but not the units.
