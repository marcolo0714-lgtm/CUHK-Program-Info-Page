Overseas Experience [b]: Students are required to complete at least one of the following: (1) a regular term-time academic exchange (organized by OAL or the Colleges); (2) a summer/winter study abroad programme. Students should complete 3 units of business-related courses from (1) or (2) with prior approval from the major programme. Academic results obtained will not be included in the calculation of the Major GPA. Under special circumstances, students who are unable to fulfil this requirement, must make up for the shortfall in units by completing appropriate courses at CUHK, subject to the approval of the major programme.

Explanatory Notes

1.

GLEF courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled with # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Double majors in GLEF and ECON, GLEF and IFAA, and GLEF and QFIN are not allowed.

3.

Students of GLEF Programme who wish to claim IBBA as second major must choose a concentration area other than the Finance concentration area.

[a]

Students are advised to take MGNT2512 once their Global Experiential Learning Activities are completed.

[b]

Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in relation to the fulfilment of the overseas experience requirement.

[c]

Subject to the approval of the major programme, students who have completed the Co-operative Education Programme are deemed to have fulfilled the capstone requirement GLEF4070.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
