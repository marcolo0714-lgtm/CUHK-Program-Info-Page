Explanatory Notes

1.

ECON courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

Depending on their academic background, holders of associate degrees are advised to take additional foundation courses beyond the 8 units required as appropriate.

[b]

Students are allowed to take ECON courses at 5000 or above level to fulfill the Elective Courses requirement, subject to the consent of the course instructor.

[c]

Students may declare a concentration area (i) after the requisite courses for the concentration area have been completed, or (ii) in the same term that the requisite courses are expected to be completed. Upon completion of all requisite courses for a concentration area, such concentration area will be indicated on academic transcripts.

[d]

Students are required to declare which MATH course is to be counted towards the requirement of the Economic Theory concentration area at their final term of attendance.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
