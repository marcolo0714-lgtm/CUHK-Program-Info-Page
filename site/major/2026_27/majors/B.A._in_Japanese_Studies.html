(b)For Standard Scheme Only. Not for Local Study Scheme18 units

Students are required to participate in one academic year (not less than eight calendar months) of resident study in Japan[c] and complete Japanese language and Japanese studies courses with prior approval from the Department of Japanese Studies before graduation. With the approval from the Department, students can apply to take the Local Study Scheme (see below) if they have strong justifications or when resident study in Japan is not possible due to unpredictable circumstances such as restrictions on travel due to epidemic outbreaks or limitations of exchange places. Units obtained from Japanese universities will be recognized as up to 18 Major units. Academic results obtained from Japanese universities will not be included in the calculation of the Major GPA. Students who are unable to obtain the required units in Japanese universities should make up the shortfall by taking courses at 4000 and above level for language courses and all levels for non-language courses after their return to this University.

Explanatory Notes

1.

JASP courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students are required to possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) level or equivalent when admitted. They are advised to take Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level in their first year of study and take Language courses at 4000 level in their second year of study. Students who have prior knowledge of Japanese (beyond Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) level) before admission should seek the advice of the Department to take substitute courses, as appropriate. Approved courses taken in substitution for other courses will be included in the Major GPA calculation. Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N1) qualification when admitted are only allowed to take courses at 4000 level for Language Courses.

3.

Students are encouraged to join summer exchange programmes organized by the Department.

4.

Students who are interested in doing fieldwork in Japan during optional exchange or conducting a comprehensive final-year project, are encouraged to seek advice from the Department for alternative study pathway.

Explanatory Notes

1.

JASP courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Subject to approval by the Major Programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major Programme only. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the 1st Major Programme as a single major. Students should be aware that such decision may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.

[a]

Students who have prior knowledge of Japanese before admission may be exempted from taking JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480. They should seek the advice of the Department to take substitute courses in accordance with their academic qualifications as follows:

[i]

Students who passed placement test and exempted from taking any or all required language course(s) (JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and/or 2480) are required to take corresponding units of Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level specified under 3(b) above, to substitute for the relevant course(s). These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

[ii]

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 12 units of Language Courses at 3000 or 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

[iii]

Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N1) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 9 units of Language Courses at 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.

[iv]

Native Japanese students are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with or without any Language Courses, to substitute for the four relevant courses. If they choose to take Language Courses, they are allowed to take Language Courses at 4000 level only, subject to the approval of the course teacher. If they choose to take non-language courses, they should take at least 12 units of elective courses at 2000 or above level.Approved courses taken in substitution for JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480 will be included in the Major GPA calculation.

[b]

Subject to Department’s approval, native speaker of Japanese can be exempted from the fulfillment of one JASP 4000 level language course, and take 3 units of JASP course as a substitution.

[c]

These courses are only offered to students before their resident study in Japan.

[d]

LING1000 which counts towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 1 Faculty Package cannot be used for the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 3(a) Non-language Courses and the Japanese Language and Linguistics stream.
