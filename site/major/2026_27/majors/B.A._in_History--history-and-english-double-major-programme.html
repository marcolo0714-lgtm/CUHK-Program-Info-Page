Explanatory Notes

1.

HIST courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Subject to approval by the Major Programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major Programme only. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the 1st Major Programme as a single major. Students should be aware that such decision may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.

[a]

Students who have completed courses ENGE1310, 1320 and 3100 will be eligible to apply for exemption of 8 units of University Core English Language Requirement courses ELTU1001, 2011 and 3011. Students are required to apply for the exemption. When exemption from a particular course is recognized, students can only be exempted from the course but not the units.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
