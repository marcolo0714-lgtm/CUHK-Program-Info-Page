Explanatory Notes

1.

CHLL courses at 2000 and above level will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

A Chinese Language and Literature Major student who pursues a second Major in Linguistics can be exempted from taking LING1002 and 3108 by taking CHLL3162 and 4161 respectively. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

3.

A Chinese Language and Literature Major student who pursues a second Major in Fine Arts can be exempted from taking FAAS2117, 3105, 3223 and 4106 by taking CHLL4403, 4332, 4121 and 4401 respectively. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.
