Explanatory Notes

1.

Students enrolled in the Double Major Programme should normally spend four regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study, except otherwise specified in 2. and 3. Below. It applies to all students, including those who select a 2nd Major offered by CUHK.

2.

Students may apply for the Co-operative Education (Co-op) Programme (5 units) in Year 2 or above. Students joining the Co-op Programme will undertake a full-time placement in two specific work terms, including one summer session and one regular term (i.e., term 1 or term 2). They have to spend at least three regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study.

3.

Students who join term-time exchange programme have to spend at least three regular terms at CUHK(SZ) during the four years of study.

4.

Students enrolled in the Double Major programme can take substitute/equivalent/alternative courses either at CUHK or CUHK(SZ). The list of substitute/equivalent/alternative courses is available at this link (https://aseidm.cdmp.cuhk.edu.hk). Students should not take a course and its substitute/equivalent/alternative course. Should they do so, only one course will be counted towards the fulfilment of units requirements. Grades of relevant substitute/equivalent/alternative courses taken at CUHK(SZ) will be transferred to CUHK for the purpose of calculation of GPA, Major GPA and credit units.

5.

Students normally have to declare their 2nd Major by the end of their first year of study.

6.

All courses at 2000 and above level and courses labeled as # in the requirements of the 1st Major Programme, as well as 3 units of research component course offered by the 2nd Major, will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

7.

Courses taken to fulfil the requirements of the 1st Major cannot be counted towards the fulfilment of requirements of the 2nd Major, and vice versa.

8.

Under very special circumstances and subject to approval by the Major programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major programme only, and they will be required to take an additional 3 units of research component course offered by the 2nd Major to fulfill the unit requirement of the 1st Major which is 60 units. The 3 units of research component will also be included in the calculation of Major GPA. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have been approved to opt out from the Double Major Programme should still observe the requirements in 1. above. If the students have not yet declared a 2nd Major at the time of opting out, they should fulfil the requirements of one of the areas under the 1st Major and take the additional 3 units of research component offered by the 2nd Major corresponding to the particular area.

9.

Students may be required to complete prerequisite courses for certain courses listed in the study scheme.

Students taking Physics offered by CUHK as 2nd Major should take PHYS1111.
