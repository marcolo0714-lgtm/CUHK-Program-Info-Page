Explanatory Notes

1.

BMBL, CHED, EDUC, HKSL, LING, SLPA, SOWK and TRAN courses at 2000 and above level as included in the Major Programme Requirement will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Subject to the Department’s approval, students can take the following HKSL courses to substitute the courses specified in 1(c):

(i) HKSL1000 and 1002 to substitute HKSL1003

(ii) HKSL2001 and 2002 to substitute HKSL2003.

3.

For students whose native language is Hong Kong Sign Language (HKSL) or who possess equivalent competence in HKSL, subject to the approval of the Major Department, they should take either an option of (a) and/or (b), or a combination of (a) to (c):

(a) courses for a modern language other than English, Chinese and HKSL

(b) courses listed under the above study scheme, and/or courses prefixed LING, with not more than six units of courses at the level of 1000

(c) up to three units of BMBL4000 taken in the first three terms of study. The exemption arrangements are specified below:

Units of HKSL Courses ExemptedCourse Substitution
3 units(a), or (b) listed above
6 units(a), and/or (b), or a combination of (a) and (b) listed above

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
