Nutritionist Stream66 units

Students admitted to the Food and Nutritional Sciences through the broad-based admission scheme may apply to specialize in the Nutritionist Stream by the end of second term in their second year of attendance. Students applying to specialize in this stream must have obtained (1) a GPA of 3.3 or above; (2) an overall result of at least 6.5 in IELTS with no less than 6.0 in each component; (3) at least a C in FNSC2005; and (4) recommendation from the selection panel. The minimum unit requirement for the Nutritionist Stream is 66. Students are required to complete a minimum of 66 units of courses as follows:

Explanatory Notes

1.

FNSC courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

[a]

Study Option: Berkeley Biosciences Study Abroad Programme Students who undergo one term of studies at the University of California, Berkeley, USA will take courses offered by the Berkeley Biosciences Study Abroad Programme. Units obtained from the Programme will be recognized as up to 12 units of Major Programme requirement. This option will be recorded on the transcript.

[b]

Students may select research-oriented course(s), as approved by the Major Programme, to substitute up to 4 units for the fulfillment of Research Courses requirement.

[c]

Students must complete any exchange/research/internship programme(s) offered by the University, Colleges, the Faculty of Science or Major Programme, as approved by the Major Programme, to fulfill the Experiential Learning requirement. Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in the exchange/research/internship programme(s).

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
