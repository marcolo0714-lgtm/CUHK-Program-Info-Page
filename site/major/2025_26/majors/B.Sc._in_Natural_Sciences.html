Faculty: Science · Admission code: NSCIN · 51 units · For students admitted in 2025–26

Students are required to complete a minimum of 51 units of courses as follows: Students must choose one concentration area and take at least 32 units of courses (including at least 3 units of research course(s)) among the subject areas prescribed under required courses and corresponding concentration area courses, namely Biological Sciences, Biotechnology, Environmental Studies, Food and Nutrition, Data Science, Chemical and Testing Sciences, and Physical Sciences. Students will fulfill this unit requirement automatically by taking the relevant courses as prescribed in each concentration area.
