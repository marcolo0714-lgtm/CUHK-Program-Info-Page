B.Sc. in Earth and Environmental Sciences — CUHK Major Requirements (2024–25)

Faculty: Science · Admission code: EESCN · 72 units · For students admitted in 2024–25

There are three streams of specialization: 1) Atmospheric Science, 2) Geology and Geophysics, and 3) Environmental Science and Technology. The stream selected by the student will be printed on the transcript. Students admitted to the Earth and Environmental Sciences Major Programme through the broad-based admission scheme may apply at their final year of attendance to specialize in any of the streams and select the relevant courses as prescribed below. Students are required to complete a minimum of 72 units of courses as follows:
