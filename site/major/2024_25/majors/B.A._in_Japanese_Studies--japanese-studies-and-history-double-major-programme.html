Explanatory Notes

1.

JASP courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Subject to approval by the Major Programmes concerned, students may opt out of the Double Major Programme and register for the 1st Major Programme only. The decision to opt out is irrevocable. Students who have opted out are required to complete and fulfill all graduation requirements by following the study scheme of the 1st Major Programme as a single major. Students should be aware that such decision may result in a delay in graduation because the requirements of the single major study scheme differ from those of the Double Major Programme.

[a]

Students who have prior knowledge of Japanese before admission may be exempted from taking JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480. They should seek the advice of the Department to take substitute courses in accordance with their academic qualifications as follows:[i] Students who passed placement test and exempted from taking any or all required language course(s) (JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and/or 2480) are required to take corresponding units of Language courses at 3000 or 4000 level specified under 3(b) above, to substitute for the relevant course(s). These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.[ii] Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N2) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 12 units of Language Courses at 3000 or 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.[iii] Students who possess Japanese Language Proficiency Test (N1) qualification when admitted are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with at least 9 units of Language Courses at 4000 level, to substitute for the four relevant courses. These substitute courses cannot be double-counted to fulfill the required elective of 3 units of 4000 level language course.[iv] Native Japanese students are required to take 18 units of courses from 3(a) and/or 3(b) above, with or without any Language Courses, to substitute for the four relevant courses. If they choose to take Language Courses, they are allowed to take Language Courses at 4000 level only, subject to the approval of the course teacher. If they choose to take non-language courses, they should take at least 12 units of elective courses at 2000 or above level.Approved courses taken in substitution for JASP1470, 1480, 2470 and 2480 will be included in the Major GPA calculation.

[b]

Subject to Department’s approval, native speaker of Japanese can be exempted from the fulfillment of one JASP 4000 level language course, and take 3 units of JASP course as a substitution.

[c]

These courses are only offered to students before their resident study in Japan.

[d]

LING1000 which counts towards the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 1 Faculty Package cannot be used for the fulfillment of the requirements specified in 3(a) Non-language Courses and the Japanese Language and Linguistics stream.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
