(b)Resident Study Overseas9 units

Students are required to study overseas for one academic term in a partner institution via an exchange programme administered by the Office of Academic Links or the Colleges, and complete at least 9 units of courses related to Global Studies approved by the Programme Director. Grades obtained in these courses are not included in the calculation of GPA. Students who are unable to obtain the required units from partner institutions must make up the shortfall by completing appropriate courses at CUHK, subject to the approval of the Programme Director.

(c)Language Courses6 units

Students are required to take 6 units of language courses other than those on Chinese (Cantonese/Putonghua) or English. Non-local students whose native language is not Chinese or English must take courses on a language that is approved by the Programme Director.

Explanatory Notes

1.

GLSD courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students may be required to participate in activities such as field trips and study tours that foster their learning of global studies.

3.

Students who participate in the Dual Undergraduate Degree Programme (DDP) must first complete at least 42 units of courses of Major requirements at CUHK. Upon completion of their studies at Waseda, students may apply to transfer up to 18 units of courses to fulfill the Major requirements at CUHK, including the 9-unit requirement of Resident Study Overseas and up to 9 units of the remaining Major requirements.

4.

Students are responsible for the extra costs incurred in relation to the overseas resident study and the participation of the DDP.

[a]

Among the non-GLSD courses, students can only choose up to three courses under the same subject area.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
