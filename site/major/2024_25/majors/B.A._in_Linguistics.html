Explanatory Notes

1.

LING courses at 2000 and above level as well as those labeled as # will be included in the calculation of Major GPA for honours classification.

2.

Students may be required to participate in activities such as field trips and study tours that foster their learning of linguistics. Students are responsible for part of or all costs incurred in relation to these activities.

3.

Students whose first language is not Chinese may take Cantonese and Putonghua as language courses for 3(b).

4.

A Linguistics Major student who pursues a second Major in Chinese Language and Literature can be exempted from taking CHLL3162, 4161 and 4110 by taking LING1002, 3108 and 4401 respectively. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

5.

A Linguistics Major student who pursues a second Major in Psychology can be exempted from taking PSYC3430 by taking LING3202. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

6.

A Linguistics Major student who pursues a second Major in Translation can be exempted from taking TRAN2610 by taking LING3401. Units earned can be counted towards both Major Programmes.

[a]

The course not taken to fulfill requirement 2(a) may be taken as a free elective course.

[^]

Old course code used in 2024-25 and before.

About this website

This is an unofficial website. For the latest information, check these official CUHK sources:

  • CUHK programme information
  • CUHK student handbook
  • CUHK course catalogue

The site brings together all undergraduate major and minor programme information published by CUHK and includes a minor progress checker. Updates are irregular, but the author aims to refresh the site before course registration periods.

Check your admission year and study scheme. Choose your year of admission and, where available, the study scheme that applies to you to see the correct requirements.

Why this site exists

  1. Gather scattered minor requirements in one place.
  2. Show essential course information alongside major and minor requirements in one view, which CUSIS does not provide, so students can choose suitable courses more easily.
  3. Help people outside CUHK explore majors they might want to pursue by browsing course titles and programme requirements.
