B.Sc. in Statistics — CUHK Major Requirements (2024–25)

Faculty: Science · Admission code: STATN · 63 units · For students admitted in 2024–25

There are three streams of specialization: the stream of Data Science and Business Statistics, the stream of Statistical Learning and Data Mining and the stream of Data Analytics. A student’s selected stream will be printed on his/her transcript. All students are under the stream of Data Science and Business Statistics unless they apply at their final year of attendance to specialize in the stream of Statistical Learning and Data Mining or the stream of Data Analytics and select the relevant courses as prescribed below. Major Programme Requirement Data Science and Business Statistics Stream Students are required to complete a minimum of 63 units of courses as follows:
