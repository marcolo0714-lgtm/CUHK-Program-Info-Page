Explanatory Notes

1.      This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Translation, Translation and Chinese Language & Literature Double Major, Translation and Linguistics Double Major Programmes and Bachelor of Laws/Bachelor of Arts (Translation) Double Degree Option.
2.      No more than 10 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major programmes and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfil the requirement of this Minor Programme.
