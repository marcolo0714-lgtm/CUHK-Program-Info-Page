Explanatory Notes

1.This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in English Major Programme, English and History Double Major Programme, History and English Double Major Programme, the Bachelor of Arts (English Studies) and Bachelor of Education (English Language Education).
2.No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
