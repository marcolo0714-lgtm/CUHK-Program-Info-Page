Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Note:

 

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Public Humanities.

2.

No more than 10 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

[a]

Courses that count towards the fulfilment of elective course requirements specified in 2(a) cannot be counted towards the fulfilment of the elective course requirements specified in 2(b).
