Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Notes:

 

1.

Students are advised not to take courses at 3000 or above level in their first year of attendance.

2.

No more than 6 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.
