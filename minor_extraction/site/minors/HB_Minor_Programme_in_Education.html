Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Notes:

 

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Chinese Language Education, Early Childhood Education, English Language Education, Exercise Science and Health Education, Human Movement Science and Health Studies, Learning Design and Technology, Mathematics and Mathematics Education, and Physical Education, Exercise Science and Health.

2.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

[a]

Courses not taken to fulfill the requirements of 1(a)(i)−(iii) can be used to fulfill the requirement of 1(b).
