Explanatory Notes

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Risk Management Science, Global Economics and Finance, Quantitative Finance and Risk Management Science, Bachelor of Business Administration (Integrated BBA Programme) and Juris Doctor Programme, and programmes under the Faculty of Business Administration.

2.

This Minor Programme is also not applicable to students who pursue the following programmes: Bachelor of Business Administration in Integrated BBA Programme and Financial Technology Double Major Programme; Bachelor of Engineering in Financial Technology and Integrated BBA Programme Double Major Programme; Bachelor of Science in Biotechnology, Entrepreneurship and Healthcare Management; and Bachelor of Science in Interdisciplinary Data Analytics & X Double Major Programme with 2nd Major in Integrated BBA Programme.

3.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

4.

Students must meet the requirement of a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.7 by the time they declare a Minor in Business.

5.

Students should note that there are pre-requisite requirements for some of the courses.
