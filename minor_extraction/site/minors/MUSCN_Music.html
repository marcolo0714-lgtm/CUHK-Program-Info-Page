Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Note:

1.

Students planning to Minor in Music must have attained at least Grade 5 in theory and instrument/voice in the examination of the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music, or equivalent, and may be asked to attend an interview.

2.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Music.

3.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfil the requirement of this Minor Programme.
