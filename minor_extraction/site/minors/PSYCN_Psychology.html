Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Notes:

1.

Entry requirement:

Students are required to take PSYC1000 before being admitted to the Minor Programme.

2.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

[a]

Students are advised to take PSYC1000. Students who have taken UGED2581 should take another PSYC course as a substitute of PSYC1000 to fulfil the Minor requirements.

[b]

Students are advised to take PSYC2010. Students who have taken any of the equivalent statistics courses including COMM3110, DOTE2011, ECON2121, GRMD2102, LING3403, PHPC2015, SEEM2430, SOCI2004, SPED2600, STAT2001 and 2006, STAT2101 and 2102 are exempted from taking PSYC2010, and they are required to take another PSYC course listed under 2. Elective Courses to compensate for the units.
