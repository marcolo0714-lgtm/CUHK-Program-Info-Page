Explanatory Notes

Explanatory Notes:

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Computational Data Science, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, the Bachelor of Engineering (Computer Engineering) and Bachelor of Business Administration (Integrated BBA programme) Double Degree Option, the Bachelor of Science (Computer Science) and Bachelor of Business Administration (Integrated BBA Programme) Double Degree Option; and students in the Mathematics-Multidisciplinary Stream of Mathematics Major Programme.

2

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major

Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

3.

Students should note that there are pre-requisite requirements for some of the courses.
