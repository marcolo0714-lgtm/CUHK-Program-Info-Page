Explanatory Notes:

 

[a]

Students who have prior knowledge of French before admission may be exempted from taking FREN2000, 2001, 2050, 3000, 3001 and 3050. They should seek the advice of the Department to take substitute courses in accordance with their academic qualifications.

[b]

Students who have taken the summer intensive course FREN2050 will be recognized as having fulfilled this requirement.

[c]

Students who have taken the summer intensive course FREN3051 will be recognized as having fulfilled this requirement and 3 units of elective courses of Requirement (2).
