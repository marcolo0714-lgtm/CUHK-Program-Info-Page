Explanatory Notes:

1.

This Minor Programme is not applicable to students who major in Integrated BBA Programme (including Double Degree/Dual Degree Programmes and Double Degree/Double Major Options) with Entrepreneurship Concentration.

2.

No more than 9 units of courses taken to fulfill the requirements of the students’ Major Programme(s) and other Minor Programme(s) respectively can be used to fulfill the requirement of this Minor Programme.

[a]

Except for EPIN courses, students should not take more than 6 units of courses offered by Departments/Schools of the same Faculty.
