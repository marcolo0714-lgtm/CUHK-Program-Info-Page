Explanatory Notes:

1.

This Minor Programme is not for students whose native language is Cantonese/Putonghua. Students planning to minor in this Programme should take the Cantonese placement tests offered by the Yale-China Chinese Language Academy.  Declaration of this Minor Programme is subject to the approval of the Department.

2.

Depending on their proficiency in Cantonese, students may be asked to take advanced level courses to substitute for the courses at the 2000 level to fulfill the Minor Programme requirement. They should seek advice from the Yale-China Chinese Language Academyfor the substitute courses for them to take. 

3.

All CLCC courses taken to fulfill this Minor programme requirement cannot be counted towards the fulfillment of the University Core Requirements.
